## EN

Subject: Cursor test: which setting to use for Figma work

Hi Balázs,

As I mentioned at standup, here is the Cursor model comparison (report attached). The short version:

- **Use Auto · Intelligence for Figma and design work.** It was as good as picking Sonnet 5.5 High by hand, a bit faster, and about a third cheaper per build ($2.51 vs $3.87 on average).
- **Don't use Auto · Cost for this.** It's cheap (about $0.40 a build), but it left placeholder text and broken layouts, and its own report said everything passed.
- Cursor's usage log shows that Auto · Balance and Auto · Intelligence both run Sonnet 5.5 High, so the quality is the same; Intelligence just used fewer tokens.
- Up to four chats in parallel worked fine. Eight at once hit the Figma rate limit on my seat.

The report has screenshots of what each setting built. I ran 17 builds over three rounds, and the whole test cost about $39. It's a small test (one task, a few runs per setting), so treat the numbers as a direction, not a benchmark.

Happy to walk you through the rest in Figma if you want.

Thanks,
Daniel
