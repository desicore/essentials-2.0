# Review 2A · S4 · Debrief · Astryx, round 1

Frame: https://www.figma.com/design/maOZqRuLdksiPwUA43qSVI/Essentials-2.0---wireframes?node-id=83-551

**Verdict: the structure is right, the scale is wrong, so fix it; no rebuild.** All the content is there: a large video, the AI summary, the checklist with yes/no and no score, the AI clips, the learners, Room display and Share. The layout is sound. The interface "feels off" mainly because the brief forced two things Astryx can't do natively:

- **24 px icons:** the Button icon frame is locked at 16 px, so Cursor added the icons to the End Content slot instead. They overflow and sit *after* the labels: "-10 s ↺", "Add photo of notes 📷", "Share ⬆".
- **44 px controls:** Astryx's largest size is 36 px. Stretching the instances to 44 px broke their inner spacing. That's the uneven spacing in the Room display control and in the tabs.

That's my brief's fault, not Astryx's. The brief and both Debrief prompts now use the library's native sizes, and a control under 44 px gets logged as a finding.

## Issues, most important first (Daniel's notes in **bold**)

1. **Icons overflow their wrappers.** These are the forced 24 px icons: the play triangles in the checklist, the marker icons, the button icons and the "Yes"/"No" circles. Go back to the native 16 px icons, with the icon before the label.
2. **The top bar and tab spacing is off.** The Room display segments and the Checklist/Annotations tabs were stretched to 44 px. Reset them to their native size (MD/LG).
3. **The font size feels too big.** The body text and the summary look like 15–16 px, and the header title (14 bold) is *smaller* than some of the body text. Use title 16–18 semibold, headings 14 semibold, body 14 and meta 12, from Astryx text styles.
4. **The timeline markers float about 80 px above the track,** with a big gap. Put them right on the progress bar: small 16 px icons sitting on the track, with the times under them in 12 px. The legend can stay.
5. **The playback speed control looks different from Room display.** It has no track background, and the selected "1×" has a stray shadow. Wrap the 4 items in a frame with the same background as the SegmentedControl, so both controls look the same.
6. **"Approve" on the clips reads as plain text** (the unpressed ToggleButton), and the "Fluids before cultures" title wraps, which knocks that card out of line. Use a Secondary Button "Approve" with a check icon, the same height in all 3 cards.
7. **The learners row touches the bottom edge** (about 8 px left). Give it the same bottom padding as the other sides.
