# Review 2B · S4 · Debrief · shadcn, round 1

Frame: https://www.figma.com/design/maOZqRuLdksiPwUA43qSVI/Essentials-2.0---wireframes?node-id=96-981

**Verdict: good direction, so this is a light fix.** It's the better layout of the two Debriefs:

- The left side is flat: the video, the timeline, the controls and the clips, with no cards.
- The right side is one full-height panel behind a divider: the learners at the top as context, then the "Summary & checklist" and "Annotations" tabs.
- The checklist has a Yes/No badge in front of each item and the jump time at the end, with no score.
- "Approve" is a real switch.

## Issues, most important first

1. **The timeline markers float above the track.** This is the same problem as 2A. Their left-right positions are correct; the vertical gap is the issue.
2. **The brief's tablet rules changed after this run started.** The run stretched every control to 44 px. The new rule is to use the native size and log anything under 44 px. The tabs needed alignment fixes because of that stretching.
3. **The section-heading and marker icons are 24 px.** The sparkles in "AI summary" and "AI clips", the checklist circle and the markers are heavier than the 14–16 px text next to them. Use 16 px.
4. **There's about 60 px of empty space under the AI clips.** Let the video placeholder grow so the left column ends level with the panel.
5. *Optional, not in the prompt:* the learners show initials only (2A showed names). That's fine in a 380 px panel.
