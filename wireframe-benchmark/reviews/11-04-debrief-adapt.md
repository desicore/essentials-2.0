# DB0 · Adapt second opinion

1. **Critical — keep a review-before-reveal gate.** Gabor’s Annotations screen marks AI-detected items “review before sharing.” On a laptop, learners may already be looking at the same screen, yet the plan opens Debrief on `AI ready`. That can expose inaccurate or sensitive feedback before faculty have checked it. Keep Ehli always on, but separate generation from learner visibility: show a neutral ready state first and require an explicit reveal after review. **This is the single biggest risk.**

2. **High — restore local camera focus.** The iPad’s Layout screen is TV-specific, but its underlying job remains: choosing which camera deserves attention. A fixed 1 + 2 grid prevents faculty from enlarging a clinically relevant detail for the group. Add a compact layout/focus control near the camera grid, with click-to-focus and a clear return to 1 + 2. This does not reintroduce send-to-TV.

3. **High — two useful iPad affordances become buried.** The three feedback categories lose their at-a-glance comparison when stacked, and short clips move below the last annotation. Faculty would miss reflection prompts and clips during a live discussion. Keep the full-width stack, but add a compact three-section index; keep “Annotations & Clips” and expose Annotations versus Short clips near the top.

4. **Medium — keep the panel at 560 px.** Its 528 px content width supports readable wrapping and five tabs, while the left side retains 832 px overall and 784 px after padding. Wider weakens the shared video; narrower makes tabs and feedback fragile. A temporary wider focus mode is reasonable for transcript review or facilitator editing, but the default should stay 560 px.

5. **Medium — choose the persistent side panel.** The equal-column option leaves roughly 660 px per column after padding and gap, so its video is smaller than the panel version, while its extra toolbar consumes height and separates navigation from content. The side panel keeps video dominant, geometry stable across tabs, and scrolling contained.
