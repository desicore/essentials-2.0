### Settings vs People (for later, not this week)

- **People is its own module.** Faculty use it weekly (groups, learners), and learner assignment in the calendar depends on it. Hiding it in Settings buries a daily task.
- **Settings = set once, admin-only:** Rooms & cameras (room setup, camera layouts, readiness check), AI subscription and usage, integrations (SSO, later LMS), organisation and branding, privacy and retention (consent, local transcription), roles and permissions.
- Rule of thumb: if faculty touch it during a normal week, it's a module; if an admin sets it up once a term, it's Settings.
