What the best apps do differently

  • Separate “live” from “who can see it.” Podia exposes Published/Open/Visible together; Circle describes audience scope with Open/Private/Secret. Podia · mobbin:94f6be70…; Circle · mobbin:ec16c800….
  • Put release timing where content is managed. Teachable exposes enrolment-relative drip settings; Kajabi keeps Draft/Publish/Drip/Lock in the module action menu. Teachable · mobbin:9ef7d498…; Kajabi · mobbin:9aa981d2….
  • Make preview identity explicit. Circle names an enrolled member; Canva offers View as learner. Teachable’s curriculum preview and SchoolAI’s activity preview illustrate narrower evidence scopes, so neither alone validates an entire cohort journey. Circle · mobbin:02f1cfb0…; Canva · mobbin:5c96453b…; Teachable · mobbin:e6757446…; SchoolAI · mobbin:56f720b3….
  • Match the first action to learner state. Teachable offers Start Lesson at 0%; Coursera offers Resume with progress. The useful pattern for Essentials is a state-aware next action. Teachable · mobbin:c04c0023…; Coursera · mobbin:18683325….
  • Provide recovery and participation context beyond the invite action. Slack exposes bounce, resend and revoke states; Podia shows Customer invited alongside audience progress. Neither capture proves an email or course was opened. Slack · mobbin:2fe9271e…; Podia · mobbin:6462b667….
  • Preserve progress and the next action across devices. Coursera’s web and iOS captures support this narrower parity claim; Udemy’s mobile-only example cannot establish web/mobile parity. Coursera · mobbin:f31fedca…; Coursera · mobbin:d76cce29…; Udemy · mobbin:89b6481f….

Best practices top companies converge on

Recommendations for Essentials inferred from the captured patterns:

  • Show course publication, audience access and item release as separate, readable states. Podia · mobbin:94f6be70…; Circle · mobbin:ec16c800…; Kajabi · mobbin:9aa981d2….
  • Offer direct invitations and a copyable link together, with the effective permission visible before sharing. Figma · mobbin:b227715d…; Circle · mobbin:e801d8ae….
  • Provide a clearly labelled learner preview close to authoring, and state what the preview simulates. Circle · mobbin:02f1cfb0…; Canva · mobbin:5c96453b….
  • Lead the learner home with one useful next action and progress, then supporting course context. Teachable · mobbin:c04c0023…; Coursera · mobbin:18683325….
  • Separate invite status from course participation, and give instructors recovery actions. Slack · mobbin:2fe9271e…; Podia · mobbin:6462b667….

Open questions for Essentials 2.0

  • Does publication apply to all groups already assigned in module 04, or can different cohorts receive different release dates? The course/item/audience layers need an explicit rule. Teachable · mobbin:9ef7d498…; Circle · mobbin:ec16c800….
  • Should day one lead with the next simulation session, required preparation, or unfinished lessons? The captured self-paced homes support a next action but do not settle a session-based priority. Teachable · mobbin:c04c0023…; Coursera · mobbin:18683325….
  • Can instructor preview impersonate a specific group and date without changing learner completion? The enrolled-member and activity previews do not prove those safeguards. Circle · mobbin:02f1cfb0…; SchoolAI · mobbin:56f720b3….
  • When Canvas owns enrolment or the learner entry point, which system owns effective access, publication and revocation? Treat the access/status patterns as inputs; no Canvas capture was retained. Circle · mobbin:e801d8ae…; Podia · mobbin:94f6be70….

  • Must the mobile portal support session joining, downloads and returning from an invite link? Matched Coursera course homes do not verify those end-to-end behaviours. Coursera · mobbin:f31fedca…; Coursera · mobbin:d76cce29….
