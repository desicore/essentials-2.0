What the best apps do differently

  • They separate inspection from commitment. Figma exposes provenance and a precise insertion action; Canva lets alternatives change a persistent preview. For Essentials, preview can be optional while selection remains persistent. Figma · refero:screen-2e0d5f12; Canva · refero:flow-8062
  • They show batch scope before committing. Teachable uses lesson/section checkboxes; PandaDoc names the selected count in its add action. Both show the inserted result, unlike the single-question creation path captured in Circle. Teachable · mobbin:e8676237-6e24-4e98-90a2-e37bfa82f60a; PandaDoc · mobbin:7833133c-7d81-4179-86a5-6ba8ce80b92c; Circle · mobbin:327fbad0-5e87-48c9-b7cf-fb0f5bcce035
  • They make the reuse relationship legible. Figma names the library behind a placed instance; Ditto confirms attachment to a component. These are stronger precedents for source identity than an insertion menu alone. They still do not prove update propagation. Figma · refero:flow-5040; Ditto · mobbin:9d28e0e2-ba12-4eb5-9a77-c3227f36b647; Notion · mobbin:b8778e0e-13d3-49a6-9f01-3680ea66249b
  • They keep structure and status together. Circle visibly moves grouped lessons; Kajabi shows release states within the outline. Sequence and release can be managed nearby while remaining distinct concepts. Circle · mobbin:0489e85e-877c-41fc-92dc-6e6e7bc9c7da; Kajabi · mobbin:5b4497cf-d3c9-445f-bff6-754fbbb1cf7c
  • They preserve useful planning detail in the outline. Mindvalley shows program and lesson time; Udemy puts type and remaining duration on activity rows. This supports showing simulation duration without repeatedly opening each scenario. Mindvalley · mobbin:99d3f73c-a804-4e4d-9b11-132afbe8ab1c; Udemy · mobbin:987d1aea-1d31-4f63-ae58-5989ee6837c7
  • They offer a route out of an empty course. Podia combines guidance and example content; Kajabi supplies an editable structure. Circle’s captured empty scaffold makes the next manual action visible but gives no library-discovery route. Podia · mobbin:6d57146d-b827-45d3-800f-d925dc03c06c; Kajabi · mobbin:8edcefcc-f539-46e6-a13a-d0ed44fa3308; Circle · mobbin:5341f9e1-8516-4a2f-b941-a3ef17a2f63f

Best practices top companies converge on

Actionable recommendations inferred from the named examples in this sample; they are not claims of universal industry consensus.

  • Provide search, source context and an optional detailed preview before adding. Figma · refero:flow-5039; Figma · refero:screen-2e0d5f12; PandaDoc · mobbin:7833133c-7d81-4179-86a5-6ba8ce80b92c
  • Persist multi-selection, show its count, commit once and confirm the destination result. Teachable · mobbin:e8676237-6e24-4e98-90a2-e37bfa82f60a; PandaDoc · mobbin:7833133c-7d81-4179-86a5-6ba8ce80b92c
  • Keep grouping and add controls in the course outline, with visible item/release states. Circle · mobbin:0489e85e-877c-41fc-92dc-6e6e7bc9c7da; Kajabi · mobbin:5b4497cf-d3c9-445f-bff6-754fbbb1cf7c; Teachable · mobbin:e84f7cdf-c9b2-4a3d-bddf-31e53e19e509

Open questions for Essentials 2.0

These are product decisions prompted by gaps in the captured evidence, not inferred platform behavior.

  • Does adding a scenario pin a version, create a copy, or link to a live master? Which course-only settings survive updates, and who approves those updates? Figma and Ditto make identity visible but do not settle propagation. Figma · refero:flow-5040; Ditto · mobbin:9d28e0e2-ba12-4eb5-9a77-c3227f36b647
  • How should duplicate scenarios, unavailable institutional items and partial batch failures behave? The successful Teachable/PandaDoc captures do not establish these cases. Teachable · mobbin:e8676237-6e24-4e98-90a2-e37bfa82f60a; PandaDoc · mobbin:7833133c-7d81-4179-86a5-6ba8ce80b92c
  • Which metadata belongs to the scenario master, which can be overridden for the course, and which is safe to show in the Learner Portal? Duration is evidenced; competency mappings and equipment carry-through are not. Mindvalley · mobbin:99d3f73c-a804-4e4d-9b11-132afbe8ab1c; MasterClass · mobbin:eeaba88c-51dc-44b0-8c15-cf8775b9c361
  • Does course sequence constrain event order or prerequisites, and does adding a scenario create any event at all? Circle demonstrates outline order; Teachable demonstrates release timing. Neither proves a simulation scheduling relationship. Circle · mobbin:0489e85e-877c-41fc-92dc-6e6e7bc9c7da; Teachable · mobbin:e84f7cdf-c9b2-4a3d-bddf-31e53e19e509
  • What reaches Canvas and the Learner Portal: a versioned scenario link, an activity shell, or only event-facing information? The learner outlines show useful hierarchy but no Canvas hand-off or instructor-only debrief separation.
