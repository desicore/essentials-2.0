Access Recordings / Course Reports → Learner portal
Research for Essentials 2.0: Semester → Courses → recordings and course reports → Learner Portal. Two connected tasks: manage sensitive session recordings, and understand course and learner outcomes. Evidence covers captured product interfaces; gaps are explicitly distinguished from absent product capabilities.

What the best apps do differently
Loom and Zoom solve different retrieval needs: Loom supports finding items across a library, while Zoom keeps several recording artifacts attached to one meeting. Essentials needs both course-wide search and an event bundle. [Loom · mobbin:9e8909a2-43b5-4007-b72d-05475a8c0af4] [Zoom · mobbin:179edc0d-797b-47fa-9999-7a3998407b80]
Vimeo distinguishes privacy from collaborator rights; Zoom adds optional time limits and passcode controls. This is richer than treating “share” as only copying a link. [Vimeo · mobbin:5262ab95-d5ee-4376-ba68-5e780dcad8a8] [Zoom · mobbin:ab3d0cfe-b7ab-40a8-8aaf-b4579be5f001]
Grain ties discussion to transcript timecodes; Loom makes AI chapter structure editable. Together they support reviewing a precise simulation moment and correcting generated navigation. [Grain · mobbin:62d0f6ef-1c16-452c-8766-b3133d5bc0c7] [Loom · mobbin:9c7d6717-30ad-411d-96a1-465961438578]
Circle emphasizes course completion, Kajabi reveals the lesson-level detail beneath a learner’s percentage, and SchoolAI explicitly surfaces outcomes/mastery. These measures answer different instructor questions and should retain their labels. [Circle · mobbin:3a9baed1-2367-4a3b-9c61-6f0c792fb0ee] [Kajabi · mobbin:1227af09-4d68-457a-a7ea-a5596e2c3e4f] [SchoolAI · mobbin:00828d09-252c-4d02-92a1-58966ebbe19d]
Mixpanel makes cohort comparisons legible through visible definitions and filters; Coursera makes a learner’s assessment grade legible through weights and component results. Neither should be collapsed into a single undifferentiated “progress” metric. [Mixpanel · mobbin:d7008305-0f24-4a04-8714-75d16b5284ff] [Coursera · mobbin:35b8baea-dca0-422b-83ee-01dd411da127]
SchoolAI’s captured download ends with a success toast; Circle’s export confirms email delivery. The report interface should state where the output will arrive, rather than leaving instructors to search for it. [SchoolAI · mobbin:82be552c-140b-4a19-95bb-037c320ca775] [Circle · mobbin:464a297d-2355-443d-8123-d61f80c4a72c]
Best practices top companies converge on
Use a searchable recording library and keep the session’s related files together; add event, learner and scenario facets as an Essentials-specific extension. [Loom · mobbin:9e8909a2-43b5-4007-b72d-05475a8c0af4] [Vimeo · refero:1429d347-5ed3-4ac6-b9d1-f906e69c23e9] [Zoom · mobbin:179edc0d-797b-47fa-9999-7a3998407b80]
Show the effective audience beside sharing actions. For sensitive debriefs, adopt explicit named/group scope and optional expiry; validate the learner-own-only policy separately. [Vimeo · mobbin:5262ab95-d5ee-4376-ba68-5e780dcad8a8] [Zoom · mobbin:ab3d0cfe-b7ab-40a8-8aaf-b4579be5f001] [Loom · mobbin:cf7c8043-a315-4bc5-a889-d3683430232c]
Keep timecoded transcript, chapters and discussion close to playback. Allow corrections to AI structure and provide a no-transcript state that preserves video review. [Grain · mobbin:62d0f6ef-1c16-452c-8766-b3133d5bc0c7] [Loom · mobbin:9c7d6717-30ad-411d-96a1-465961438578] [Vimeo · mobbin:bd482934-e96c-4560-967f-2509d3c341d8]
Provide course overview, learner detail and export in a consistent reporting context. The complete path is a synthesis from complementary examples, not a single observed journey. [Circle · mobbin:464a297d-2355-443d-8123-d61f80c4a72c] [Kajabi · mobbin:1227af09-4d68-457a-a7ea-a5596e2c3e4f] [SchoolAI · mobbin:82be552c-140b-4a19-95bb-037c320ca775]
Label completion, assessed score, mastery and engagement separately; preserve metric definitions and comparison filters. Do not use video views or retained users as a mastery score. [Circle · mobbin:3a9baed1-2367-4a3b-9c61-6f0c792fb0ee] [SchoolAI · mobbin:00828d09-252c-4d02-92a1-58966ebbe19d] [Mixpanel · mobbin:d7008305-0f24-4a04-8714-75d16b5284ff] [Coursera · mobbin:35b8baea-dca0-422b-83ee-01dd411da127]
Give learners a compact progress view with a route to assessment detail. This recommendation is supported by Coursera alone; broader learner-portal convergence remains unverified. [Coursera · mobbin:93beef1d-a7ba-4f6c-9efb-4d13aee3eb76] [Coursera · mobbin:35b8baea-dca0-422b-83ee-01dd411da127]
Open questions for Essentials 2.0
Recording ownership: when several learners share a simulation, does “own recording” mean the whole event, a participant-specific clip, or an instructor-approved release? Zoom’s meeting bundle and Vimeo’s named access do not settle that mapping. [Zoom · mobbin:179edc0d-797b-47fa-9999-7a3998407b80] [Vimeo · mobbin:5262ab95-d5ee-4376-ba68-5e780dcad8a8]
Release and expiry: should feedback, transcript, comments and downloads inherit the video audience, and what happens at semester end? Zoom exposes these settings, but the captures do not establish their dependencies. [Zoom · mobbin:ab3d0cfe-b7ab-40a8-8aaf-b4579be5f001]
AI annotations: who approves chapter labels and timecoded feedback before learners see them, and how should these link to the competencies defined in module 03? Loom and Grain establish editable/navigation patterns, not assessment validity. [Loom · mobbin:9c7d6717-30ad-411d-96a1-465961438578] [Grain · mobbin:62d0f6ef-1c16-452c-8766-b3133d5bc0c7]
Comparability: how will attendance be sourced, retries handled, and scenario/rubric versions normalized across cohorts? SchoolAI labels mastery and Mixpanel exposes comparison controls; neither capture defines Elevate’s assessment rules. [SchoolAI · mobbin:00828d09-252c-4d02-92a1-58966ebbe19d] [Mixpanel · mobbin:d7008305-0f24-4a04-8714-75d16b5284ff]
Learner Portal and Canvas hand-off: which released grade, recording link and competency result is authoritative, when is it synchronized, and how are regraded results reconciled? Coursera’s personal grades and SchoolAI’s export show useful endpoints but no Canvas integration. Delivery belongs to module 09. [Coursera · mobbin:35b8baea-dca0-422b-83ee-01dd411da127] [SchoolAI · mobbin:82be552c-140b-4a19-95bb-037c320ca775]

Part A · Recording organisation, access and playback
Q1Recording organisation (by event / by learner / by scenario) and search
Loom combines broad library search, folders and metadata-rich recording cards; Vimeo emphasizes recent videos and a persistent search field. Zoom contributes the strongest event-level grouping: several recording files sit under one named meeting. For Essentials, combine this session bundle with searchable event, learner and scenario metadata. That three-axis organization is a proposal, not an observed capability in these captures. Evidence: Loom [mobbin:9e8909a2-43b5-4007-b72d-05475a8c0af4], Vimeo [refero:1429d347-5ed3-4ac6-b9d1-f906e69c23e9], Zoom [mobbin:179edc0d-797b-47fa-9999-7a3998407b80].

Q2Access control on recordings (learner sees own only, group, time-limited)
Vimeo separates link privacy from named collaborator roles. Zoom exposes audience settings, a passcode and an optional expiration checkbox, shown unchecked; its sampled flow reaches an updated state. Loom's captured 'Anyone with the link' state is a counter-example for sensitive debrief footage, not proof of its universal default. Essentials should make the recording audience explicit and reviewable. Automatic learner-own-only binding and course-group rules remain unproven in this evidence. Q4Report structure: overview → per-learner drill-down → export
The complete reporting journey is assembled from complementary evidence: Circle shows course summaries, a student table and sampled export; Kajabi shows a learner/course/module/lesson detail hierarchy; SchoolAI places class outcomes and learner insights beside CSV download. Essentials should preserve course and filter context through each level, with export scoped to the displayed report; that scope preservation is a recommendation. Q5Metrics shown by default (completion, attendance, score, mastery) and comparability across cohorts
Completion and mastery are distinct in the sample: Circle reports average course completion, Kajabi lesson completion, and SchoolAI explicitly labels outcome/mastery measures (Q4 references). Mixpanel makes metric definitions, date ranges, filters and cohort breakdowns visible, but its retention and event counts measure behavior rather than learning. Coursera's Grades screen provides assessment scores (Q6). Borrow comparison controls while keeping attendance, completion, score and mastery separate; attendance defaults and a common cross-cohort assessment basis are evidence gaps. These captures do not show learner recording access or establish a shared instructor/learner data model. Learner-side coverage is one product, so cross-product convergence is not claimed here.
