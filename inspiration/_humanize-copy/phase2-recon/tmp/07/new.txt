What the best apps do differently
Separate “live” from “who can see it.” Podia exposes Published/Open/Visible together; Circle describes audience scope with Open/Private/Secret. Podia · mobbin:94f6be70…; Circle · mobbin:ec16c800….
Put release timing where content is managed. Teachable exposes enrolment-relative drip settings; Kajabi keeps Draft/Publish/Drip/Lock in the module action menu. Teachable · mobbin:9ef7d498…; Kajabi · mobbin:9aa981d2….
Make preview identity explicit. Circle names an enrolled member; Canva offers View as learner. Teachable’s curriculum preview and SchoolAI’s activity preview illustrate narrower evidence scopes, so neither alone validates an entire cohort journey. Circle · mobbin:02f1cfb0…; Canva · mobbin:5c96453b…; Teachable · mobbin:e6757446…; SchoolAI · mobbin:56f720b3….
Match the first action to learner state. Teachable offers Start Lesson at 0%; Coursera offers Resume with progress. The useful pattern for Essentials is a state-aware next action. Teachable · mobbin:c04c0023…; Coursera · mobbin:18683325….
Provide recovery and participation context beyond the invite action. Slack exposes bounce, resend and revoke states; Podia shows Customer invited alongside audience progress. Neither capture proves an email or course was opened. Slack · mobbin:2fe9271e…; Podia · mobbin:6462b667….
Preserve progress and the next action across devices. Coursera’s web and iOS captures support this narrower parity claim; Udemy’s mobile-only example cannot establish web/mobile parity. Coursera · mobbin:f31fedca…; Coursera · mobbin:d76cce29…; Udemy · mobbin:89b6481f….
Best practices top companies converge on
Recommendations for Essentials inferred from the captured patterns:
Show course publication, audience access and item release as separate, readable states. Podia · mobbin:94f6be70…; Circle · mobbin:ec16c800…; Kajabi · mobbin:9aa981d2….
Offer direct invitations and a copyable link together, with the effective permission visible before sharing. Figma · mobbin:b227715d…; Circle · mobbin:e801d8ae….
Provide a clearly labelled learner preview close to authoring, and state what the preview simulates. Circle · mobbin:02f1cfb0…; Canva · mobbin:5c96453b….
Lead the learner home with one useful next action and progress, then supporting course context. Teachable · mobbin:c04c0023…; Coursera · mobbin:18683325….
Separate invite status from course participation, and give instructors recovery actions. Slack · mobbin:2fe9271e…; Podia · mobbin:6462b667….
Open questions for Essentials 2.0
Does publication apply to all groups already assigned in module 04, or can different cohorts receive different release dates? The course/item/audience layers need an explicit rule. Teachable · mobbin:9ef7d498…; Circle · mobbin:ec16c800….
Should day one lead with the next simulation session, required preparation, or unfinished lessons? The captured self-paced homes support a next action but do not settle a session-based priority. Teachable · mobbin:c04c0023…; Coursera · mobbin:18683325….
Can instructor preview impersonate a specific group and date without changing learner completion? The enrolled-member and activity previews do not prove those safeguards. Circle · mobbin:02f1cfb0…; SchoolAI · mobbin:56f720b3….
When Canvas owns enrolment or the learner entry point, which system owns effective access, publication and revocation? Treat the access/status patterns as inputs; no Canvas capture was retained. Circle · mobbin:e801d8ae…; Podia · mobbin:94f6be70….
What counts as sharing success: invite accepted, first authenticated course access, or preparation started? Keep these events distinct, with report access in 08 and messages/material delivery in 09. Slack · mobbin:2fe9271e…; Podia · mobbin:6462b667….
Must the mobile portal support session joining, downloads and returning from an invite link? Matched Coursera course homes do not verify those end-to-end behaviours. Coursera · mobbin:f31fedca…; Coursera · mobbin:d76cce29….
Evidence coverage and method
Mobbin deep web/iOS screen and flow searches plus Refero HTTP-helper searches. Only retained, locally downloaded screenshots support the findings; ordered flows are limited to eight captured steps. Comparison cells describe this corpus, not exhaustive or current feature availability. Flow strips may be excerpts of a longer journey. No observed example proves a complete instructor-to-invite-to-first-login journey across products. The final set has 28 references across 12 products, below the 30–50 target after relevance and image checks. Canvas, Google Classroom, Moodle, Thinkific and Maven remain evidence gaps, not negative feature findings. Refero was searched but most overlapping examples were removed by the one-source-per-product/question merge rule; the retained Gumroad screen is explicitly an adjacent counter-example.
The requested three Luna fetch threads were used. The runtime then rejected a new Sol thread with “agent thread limit reached”; Luna threads produced screenshot evidence notes and Astra synthesised them. The supplied report builder was reused without editing the previous module; this synthesis and reference anchors were added afterward. Q1Publish model (single toggle vs. per-item visibility vs. scheduled release)

The captures do not establish the consequences of unpublishing an active course.

Q2Access grant paths (enrolled groups / link / code / email) and how permission levels are expressed
Grant access in the context of the course. Circle [mobbin:e801d8ae-1e7e-45e3-9035-0452816271e5] shows invitations with selected-space access and privileged-role controls. Figma [mobbin:b227715d-bc5d-4655-9133-921c156f6837] combines email invitations, copy link and visible permissions; Figma [mobbin:ffaf7085-3f32-450d-938b-812274011f53] adds Anyone access, View/Edit and password protection. Notion [mobbin:44650693-5283-421a-b978-d4cf1a8de987] names a Member role in an email invitation. These are access patterns, not a prescribed role model for learners. A password is not evidence of a classroom join-code workflow; enrolled-group inheritance and class codes remain unverified in this set. Podia [mobbin:157502c1-8bd1-4fd3-aae9-97358b725cad] makes a 30-day trial limit explicit before inviting a customer. Gumroad [refero:11b3c0a4-eb09-4d98-9a1a-742439189540] is a creator-side counter-example: Share and Collaborators navigation do not establish a learner-access workflow.

Q3"Preview as learner" availability and fidelity
Preview has different scopes. Circle [mobbin:02f1cfb0-ef6b-4f9f-8218-bfe24c660981] explicitly labels preview as an enrolled member and retains lesson content and a completion action. Canva [mobbin:5c96453b-0c58-434f-94ba-c6d68a8f5bd5] places View as learner beside the course editor. Teachable [mobbin:e6757446-af84-43d5-bed0-da79c9a3826c] exposes curriculum preview without proving the same role simulation. SchoolAI [mobbin:56f720b3-daec-4967-9fc4-60a7f8501c55] previews an AI activity as a student, which is narrower than a whole course portal. For Essentials, label the simulated learner and group; confirm separately whether release dates, prerequisites and progress writes are simulated. Those behaviours are not established by the screenshots.

Q5Feedback to instructor that sharing worked (who has opened, pending invites)
Publishing, inviting, joining and learning are different events. Podia [mobbin:6462b667-d688-4634-a413-c914bdc3b20a] shows Customer invited alongside audience rows and progress; this supports an action confirmation and participation context, not email-open telemetry. Slack [mobbin:2fe9271e-36ed-4333-b809-6078dc790098] exposes Pending and Accepted tabs plus bounced, resent and revoked invitation states. Pending does not prove delivery. For Essentials, show the recipient, current access and available recovery action, then distinguish invitation status from first course access and learning progress. No retained capture demonstrates a course-open event. Canva [mobbin:a906866c-f694-41fd-b437-b086430c1585] confirms the course is ready, reports team notification and offers Classwork and Copy link; it does not enumerate learner acceptance.
