Announcements / Send learning materials or send to Canvas → Shareable link
Essentials 2.0 · Design research · 09-announcements-send-materials
Announcements / Send learning materials or send to Canvas → Shareable link
Internal board for the Essentials 2.0 solution concept (Semester → Courses → Announcements / send materials → Shareable link). Not about UI: each reference shows how another product lets someone running a course communicate from inside it: announce to everyone or a group, send selected files or links ahead of a session, choose the channel (portal feed, email, shareable link, Canvas LMS), schedule it, and see who received or opened it. One section per question; every card says what we would take from it. Counter-examples are flagged. Raw material from Mobbin and Refero, to be cut down in the converge pass before anything is shown outside the team.
Synthesis
Read this first. Sections Q1–Q6 below hold the evidence cards; every product and reference id named here is in the report. Ids are shortened to their first block (for example mobbin:3135010e).
Top examples
The Leap (Refero, refero:4564) — one flow from recipient segment to confirmation to live open/click stats: the whole loop a course announcement needs, in eight steps.
Google Classroom (Refero, refero:19605564) — the audience dropdown ("For · All students") sits on the composer itself; the canonical LMS composer.
beehiiv (Mobbin, mobbin:748e794e) — one post object with Post / Email / Website tabs and a per-channel recipient summary before Send.
Basecamp (Mobbin, mobbin:0bc24c79) — separates "who should see this" from "who gets notified" inside one composer.
Mural (Mobbin, mobbin:836e980f) — a single access panel with access level, visitor-link level, password, timezone-stamped expiry and Reset link.
Navan (Mobbin, mobbin:9d8af34f) — per-person status table where you select the non-responders and "Send a reminder" without leaving it.
SchoolAI (Mobbin, mobbin:3135010e) — the only complete LMS hand-off: Export → permission → class picker → "Exported to Google Classroom!".
Microsoft Teams (Mobbin, mobbin:88ad5199) — "Announcement" is a distinct post type with a bold title and replies attached; learners can tell it from chatter.

What the best apps do differently
They separate visibility from notification. Basecamp asks "who should see this?" and, separately, "when I post this, notify…" (mobbin:0bc24c79); beehiiv reconciles channels into a per-channel recipient count before Send (mobbin:748e794e). Substack collapses email and push into one default-on toggle (mobbin:a7ebc4ec), the simplest version of the same idea. Nobody in the set exposes an LMS as a channel inside the composer.
Scheduling is a property of the send button, not a feature. Slack's send dropdown with presets (mobbin:fd356e91), Whop's inline popover (mobbin:16eb85ed), Kit's send-time field that defaults to now (mobbin:f722d4bb), LinkedIn's clock icon (refero:863). Only Confluence adds an end date (mobbin:fcc604e7), the closest thing to "show this until the session".
Audience targeting is coarser than expected. Google Classroom offers "All students" or a selection (refero:19605564), Teachable everyone / course / individual (refero:9617), The Leap named segments (refero:4564). ClassDojo has no audience control at all because the class is the audience (mobbin:53e9bdad).
Feedback only matters when it is per person and has an action next to it. Navan selects non-responders and reminds them from the same table (mobbin:9d8af34f); Superhuman shows who opened, on which device, when, inline on the thread (mobbin:ba56c2de); HoneyBook branches the follow-up on "viewed within 1 day" (mobbin:8afb0d65); Docusign configures reminder cadence (mobbin:e4a526c1). Aggregate dashboards (HubSpot mobbin:5814d24c, Dub refero:2fe44823) and Square's empty "Seen by" column (mobbin:1c8c3422) give a course manager nothing to act on.
Link controls converge on one panel with five questions. Who can open, at what level, until when, with which password, how to revoke: Mural (mobbin:836e980f), Proton (mobbin:ef4fcd7f), Notion with relative presets resolved to a date (mobbin:cfca14fb). Loom is the only one that puts view tracking beside the share (refero:1242). ClickUp (mobbin:7f248abd) is the counter-example: expiry defaults to Never, no level, revoke is Unpublish.
The LMS hand-off maps to a specific class and confirms. SchoolAI is the only complete example (mobbin:3135010e). Course platforms treat publishing as the send and never hand off (Kajabi mobbin:9aa981d2); generic connectors stop at authorization (Craft mobbin:e9079303, Cofounder mobbin:7897406d, counter-example).
On the learner side, announcements get their own type or place. Teams' "Announcement" post type (mobbin:88ad5199), Circle's separate Announcements space (mobbin:c1b1c3ad), ClassDojo's teacher-only Pin (mobbin:3edf67a5); unread state aggregated centrally in Slack Activity (mobbin:8e65f9c9); channel preference per category in Spotify (refero:471b8f0c). Coursera's undifferentiated Latest list is the counter-example (mobbin:36db9592).
Best practices top companies converge on
One composer for announcements and material sends; the post in the portal is the record, channels (email, push, link, LMS) are how people hear about it.
Ask two questions, not one: who can see this (course / group / individuals) and who gets notified now.
Put "send now / schedule" on the send button with presets; add an optional end date for time-boxed material.
Attach materials by reference from the course library, not by re-upload (no product in the set does this; it is the gap Essentials can fill).
Give the shareable link one panel: who, level, expiry (relative presets resolved to a date), password, revoke; show opens next to the link.
Report delivery per person with an action beside it (remind non-openers, optionally automatic before the session); aggregate counts are secondary.
For the LMS hand-off: say what will be sent, map to one specific course, confirm with a toast, and keep the link back.
On the learner side: a distinct announcement type, pinnable, with attachments and replies inline, and a central unread inbox that agrees with email.
Open questions for Essentials 2.0
Audience unit. The set targets by segment or course. For a simulation course the natural units are the course roster, the learner groups from sub-module 04, and the session cohort from 06; faculty and observers are a fourth audience nobody in the set models.
Materials by reference. Every composer uploads. If materials live in sub-module 02, an announcement should link to the library item, inherit its access, and show whether it is already released; no reference shows this.
Portal as source of truth. If the Learner Portal feed is the record, email and push are notifications of the same post, and the shareable link is a login-free view of it. That ties directly to the access-sharing module (magic links, time-boxed access); the link controls in Q3 should reuse those rules rather than add a second permission system.
Canvas hand-off object. SchoolAI sends a document to one class. For Canvas the open questions are what object is created (announcement, module item, or a link back to the portal), how an Essentials course maps to a Canvas course when they differ, what happens on edit after send, and whether the learner then has two places to look. No reference shows edit-after-send or re-sync.
"Seen" in education. Superhuman-style per-person opens are useful before a simulation (did they read the pre-brief?) but may feel like surveillance to learners. Whether reminders are manual (Navan) or automatic before the session (Docusign / HoneyBook) is a product decision, not a pattern the set settles.
Scheduling against the session. Products schedule by date/time. Essentials could schedule relative to a session ("two days before", "after debrief"), which none of the references model.

Q1Composer: audience targeting (course / group / individual), attachment of course materials, scheduling
Every composer in the set treats scheduling as a property of the send action, not a separate feature: Slack's send-button dropdown with presets and a custom date/time (mobbin:fd356e91), Whop's inline calendar popover (mobbin:16eb85ed), LinkedIn's clock icon (refero:863), Basecamp's 'Schedule and save' (mobbin:25c42c82). Audience targeting is rarer and coarser than the question assumes: Google Classroom puts a 'For / All students' dropdown directly above the text box (refero:19605564), Teachable offers everyone / a course / an individual (refero:9617), The Leap uses described segments (refero:4564), and Confluence only Public/Private plus a start–end window (mobbin:fcc604e7). Attachments are inline uploads (ClassDojo photo, Basecamp PDF, Whop image); nobody attaches from a course library. Only The Leap closes the loop from recipients through confirmation to open/click stats. Confluence's one-active-banner cap is the caveat: a persistent materials banner and a timely one cannot coexist.

Q2Channel choice (in-app feed, email, push, link, external LMS) and whether it is one composer or many features
Two models. 'One composer, many channels' puts the channel as a control on the same post: beehiiv's Post / Email / Website / Delivery tabs reconcile into a per-channel recipient summary before sending (mobbin:748e794e); Kit layers a 'Publish to web' opt-in over a default email send (mobbin:f722d4bb); Intercom folds platform (web / iOS / Android) and push into one panel (mobbin:7380bc5c). 'Implicit channel' treats the in-app post as the channel and only asks who is notified: Basecamp separates 'who should see this' from 'when I post this, notify…' (mobbin:0bc24c79), and Substack bundles email and push into a single default-on 'notify subscribers' toggle (mobbin:a7ebc4ec). Nobody in the set exposes an external LMS as a channel inside the composer; that lives in an export menu (see Q5). The useful distinction for a course is visibility versus notification: the post is the record, the channels are how people hear about it.

Q3Shareable link controls (expiry, access level, revoke, tracking)
Link controls converge on one panel with five questions: who can open (anyone / workspace / specific people), at what level (view / comment / edit), until when, with which password, and how to revoke. Mural shows all five, including a timezone-stamped expiry and a 'Reset visitor link' that regenerates rather than merely disables (mobbin:836e980f); Proton has expiry, password and 'Stop sharing' (mobbin:ef4fcd7f); Notion resolves relative presets ('in a week') to an absolute date inline and ends with a red Remove (mobbin:cfca14fb). Coda (mobbin:c333ff8e), Dropbox (mobbin:777c4ef0) and Sketch (mobbin:1fb17c11) focus on tiered access (workspace / guests / everyone) and per-person levels, with Copy link always in the same corner; Sketch splits public rights into download versus comment checkboxes. Only Loom shows tracking next to the share ('No views yet', refero:1242). ClickUp is the counter-example (mobbin:7f248abd): expiry defaults to Never with no presets, no access level, and the only revoke is an all-or-nothing Unpublish.

Q4Delivery feedback (sent / delivered / seen, reminders)
Feedback is worth having only when it is per person and has an action next to it. Navan lists each invitee with a status, lets you select the non-responders and 'Send a reminder' from the same table (mobbin:9d8af34f); Superhuman shows per-person, per-device, timestamped opens inline on the thread itself (mobbin:ba56c2de). Automation goes further: HoneyBook branches the follow-up on 'file viewed within 1 day' (mobbin:8afb0d65); Docusign lets you set the days before the first reminder and the repeat interval (mobbin:e4a526c1). Newsletter tools lead with aggregate counters (Mailchimp mobbin:6db18da5, HubSpot mobbin:5814d24c; Dub for link clicks, refero:2fe44823) and keep per-recipient detail one tab away (Outseta's Recipients tab with last-opened timestamps, mobbin:34451b74). WhatsApp's Delivered / Read is the minimum: two states, one recipient (mobbin:5580e66f). Square's Announcements list is the caution (mobbin:1c8c3422): a 'Seen by' column that shows nothing and offers no follow-up action.

Q5External LMS hand-off UX (auth, mapping to course, what is sent, confirmation)
The thinnest question in the set and the most important one for Essentials. Only SchoolAI shows a complete hand-off (mobbin:3135010e): an Export menu that lists Google Classroom next to Docs / PDF, a permission modal that says what will be shared, an OAuth enable step, a Class picker that maps the document to one specific class, and an 'Exported to Google Classroom!' toast. Course platforms in the set have no hand-off at all: in Kajabi, publishing inside the product is the send, so the learner-facing surface is the product's own portal (mobbin:9aa981d2). Generic connectors stop at authorization: Craft is a Connect / Disconnect toggle on a settings page (mobbin:e9079303), and Cofounder's OAuth flow with per-scope toggles never reaches a 'what was sent' or success state (mobbin:7897406d, counter-example). Nobody shows editing or re-syncing after the hand-off.

Q6Learner-side reception (feed, notification, email) and consistency with the portal
The consistent products give announcements a distinct type or place on the learner side. Microsoft Teams offers 'Post' versus 'Announcement' in the composer and renders announcements with a bold title and replies attached (mobbin:88ad5199); Circle routes them to an 'Announcements' space separate from the feed (mobbin:c1b1c3ad); ClassDojo keeps a teacher-only Pin so the post stays on top, with the attachment and replies inline (mobbin:3edf67a5). Unread state is aggregated centrally in Slack's Activity screen rather than only inside the source channel (mobbin:8e65f9c9), and Spotify lets the recipient choose email versus push per category, a model for learner notification preferences (refero:471b8f0c). Coursera is the counter-example (mobbin:36db9592): instructor content sits in a 'Latest'-sorted Q&A list with no pin, no unread marker and no sender role, so an announcement is indistinguishable from peer chatter.
