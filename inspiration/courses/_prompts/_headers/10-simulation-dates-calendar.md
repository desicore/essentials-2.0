# Research prompt — Courses › Simulation Dates (Calendar) → Invitations

Using Mobbin & Refero MCP, research how top apps design **the course calendar and invitation flow** (calendar views of scheduled sessions, communicating dates to learners, calendar invitations / ICS / sync to Google or Outlook, RSVP and reminders, and pushing dates to an external LMS like Canvas). Find the most relevant screens and user flows, compare patterns across products, and identify what the best apps do differently. Create a visual HTML report (images embedded directly) showing the top examples, common patterns, and the best practices top companies converge on. Keep the big picture in mind: this is one sub-module of **managing courses** in Essentials 2.0 (Semester → Courses → this → Invitations).

## What the instructor is trying to do here
See all simulation dates for a course (and across courses in the semester), spot conflicts, communicate dates to learners and facilitators (invitations with RSVP, reminders, changes), let learners sync to their own calendars, and optionally publish the dates to Canvas. Creating a single event is sub-module 06 — here focus on the **overview, communication and invitation** layer.

## Search queries to start from
Mobbin `search_screens` (web, deep):
- "calendar month and week view with events colour coded by course"
- "event invitation email or page with RSVP yes no maybe and add to calendar buttons"
- "upcoming sessions list for a student with join and add to calendar actions"
- "calendar sync settings with Google Calendar and Outlook connect buttons"
- "reschedule event dialog with notify attendees option"
Mobbin `search_flows` (web, then ios):
- "organizer sends calendar invitations to attendees and tracks RSVPs"
- "user connects Google Calendar and subscribes to a course schedule"
- "rescheduling an event and notifying participants of the change"
Refero: same intents; also Google Calendar, Notion Calendar, Cal.com, Calendly, Luma, Eventbrite, Zoom, Canvas Calendar, Google Classroom (Calendar integration), Maven (session reminders).

Products to prioritise: Google Calendar, Notion Calendar, Cal.com, Calendly, Luma, Eventbrite, Zoom, Canvas, Google Classroom, Moodle, Maven, Docebo, Teachable / Thinkific live events.

## Questions the board answers (Q1–Q6: these become the H2 sections of the report and the columns of the comparison table)
1. Calendar views offered (month / week / agenda / per-course filter) and cross-course overview
2. Invitation model (per event vs. subscribe-to-course feed; ICS / native sync)
3. RSVP and attendance capture; capacity handling
4. Change management (reschedule → notify → updated invites) and reminder cadence
5. Learner-side calendar experience (portal + personal calendar parity)
6. External LMS / calendar hand-off (Canvas, Google, Outlook) UX
