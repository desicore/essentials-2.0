What the best products do differently

  • They separate inspection from commitment. Figma shows source info and a precise insertion action; Canva lets alternatives change a persistent preview. For Essentials, preview can be optional while selection stays as people browse. Figma · refero:screen-2e0d5f12; Canva · refero:flow-8062
  • They show batch scope before committing. Teachable uses lesson and section checkboxes; PandaDoc names the selected count in its add action. Both show the inserted result, unlike the single-question creation path in Circle. Teachable · mobbin:e8676237-6e24-4e98-90a2-e37bfa82f60a; PandaDoc · mobbin:7833133c-7d81-4179-86a5-6ba8ce80b92c; Circle · mobbin:327fbad0-5e87-48c9-b7cf-fb0f5bcce035
  • They make the reuse relationship clear. Figma names the library behind a placed linked copy; Ditto confirms attachment to a component. Those are stronger precedents for source identity than an insertion menu alone. They still do not prove that updates spread. Figma · refero:flow-5040; Ditto · mobbin:9d28e0e2-ba12-4eb5-9a77-c3227f36b647; Notion · mobbin:b8778e0e-13d3-49a6-9f01-3680ea66249b
  • They keep structure and status together. Circle visibly moves grouped lessons; Kajabi shows release states within the outline. Sequence and release can sit nearby while remaining separate ideas. Circle · mobbin:0489e85e-877c-41fc-92dc-6e6e7bc9c7da; Kajabi · mobbin:5b4497cf-d3c9-445f-bff6-754fbbb1cf7c
  • They keep useful planning detail in the outline. Mindvalley shows program and lesson time; Udemy puts type and remaining duration on activity rows. That supports showing simulation duration without opening each scenario again. Mindvalley · mobbin:99d3f73c-a804-4e4d-9b11-132afbe8ab1c; Udemy · mobbin:987d1aea-1d31-4f63-ae58-5989ee6837c7
  • They offer a route out of an empty course. Podia combines guidance and example content; Kajabi supplies an editable structure. Circle's empty scaffold makes the next manual action visible but gives no library path. Podia · mobbin:6d57146d-b827-45d3-800f-d925dc03c06c; Kajabi · mobbin:8edcefcc-f539-46e6-a13a-d0ed44fa3308; Circle · mobbin:5341f9e1-8516-4a2f-b941-a3ef17a2f63f

What the leading products have in common

These recommendations come from the examples cited. They are design proposals for Essentials 2.0, not claims of industry-wide agreement.

  • Provide search, source context and an optional detailed preview before adding. Figma · refero:flow-5039; Figma · refero:screen-2e0d5f12; PandaDoc · mobbin:7833133c-7d81-4179-86a5-6ba8ce80b92c
  • Keep multi-selection, show its count, commit once and confirm the destination result. Teachable · mobbin:e8676237-6e24-4e98-90a2-e37bfa82f60a; PandaDoc · mobbin:7833133c-7d81-4179-86a5-6ba8ce80b92c
  • Keep grouping and add controls in the course outline, with visible item and release states. Circle · mobbin:0489e85e-877c-41fc-92dc-6e6e7bc9c7da; Kajabi · mobbin:5b4497cf-d3c9-445f-bff6-754fbbb1cf7c; Teachable · mobbin:e84f7cdf-c9b2-4a3d-bddf-31e53e19e509
  • Show activity duration and type where instructors review the sequence; check instructor and learner details separately. Mindvalley · mobbin:99d3f73c-a804-4e4d-9b11-132afbe8ab1c; Udemy · mobbin:987d1aea-1d31-4f63-ae58-5989ee6837c7
  • Offer an editable starting structure and light guidance, with the reusable library as Essentials' primary entry action. Podia · mobbin:6d57146d-b827-45d3-800f-d925dc03c06c; Kajabi · mobbin:8edcefcc-f539-46e6-a13a-d0ed44fa3308

Open questions for Essentials 2.0

These are product decisions prompted by gaps in the screenshots, not inferred platform behaviour.

  • Does adding a scenario keep a fixed version, create a copy, or link to a live original? Which course-only settings survive updates, and who approves those updates? Figma and Ditto make identity visible but do not settle whether updates spread. Figma · refero:flow-5040; Ditto · mobbin:9d28e0e2-ba12-4eb5-9a77-c3227f36b647
  • How should duplicate scenarios, unavailable institutional items and partial batch failures behave? The successful Teachable and PandaDoc screenshots do not cover these cases. Teachable · mobbin:e8676237-6e24-4e98-90a2-e37bfa82f60a; PandaDoc · mobbin:7833133c-7d81-4179-86a5-6ba8ce80b92c
  • Which details belong to the original scenario, which can be overridden for the course, and which are safe to show in the Learner Portal? Duration is shown; competency mappings and equipment carry-through are not. Mindvalley · mobbin:99d3f73c-a804-4e4d-9b11-132afbe8ab1c; MasterClass · mobbin:eeaba88c-51dc-44b0-8c15-cf8775b9c361
  • Does course sequence constrain event order or prerequisites, and does adding a scenario create any event at all? Circle shows outline order; Teachable shows release timing. Neither proves a simulation scheduling relationship. Circle · mobbin:0489e85e-877c-41fc-92dc-6e6e7bc9c7da; Teachable · mobbin:e84f7cdf-c9b2-4a3d-bddf-31e53e19e509
  • What reaches Canvas and the Learner Portal: a versioned scenario link, a simple activity placeholder, or only event-facing information? The learner outlines show useful hierarchy but no Canvas hand-off or instructor-only debrief separation. Udemy · mobbin:045c215d-6e08-432c-9bb6-3ddd4495fc69; Udemy · mobbin:987d1aea-1d31-4f63-ae58-5989ee6837c7
