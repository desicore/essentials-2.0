What the best products do differently

  • Separate "live" from "who can see it." Podia shows 'Published'/'Open'/'Visible' together; Circle describes who can see it with 'Open'/'Private'/'Secret'. Podia · mobbin:94f6be70…; Circle · mobbin:ec16c800….
  • Put release timing where content is managed. Teachable shows enrolment-relative drip settings; Kajabi keeps 'Draft'/'Publish'/'Drip'/'Lock' in the module action menu. Teachable · mobbin:9ef7d498…; Kajabi · mobbin:9aa981d2….
  • Make preview identity explicit. Circle names an enrolled member; Canva offers 'View as learner'. Teachable's curriculum preview and SchoolAI's activity preview show narrower cases, so neither alone validates an entire cohort journey. Circle · mobbin:02f1cfb0…; Canva · mobbin:5c96453b…; Teachable · mobbin:e6757446…; SchoolAI · mobbin:56f720b3….
  • Match the first action to where the learner is. Teachable offers 'Start Lesson' at 0%; Coursera offers 'Resume' with progress. The useful pattern for Essentials is a next action that fits the learner's progress. Teachable · mobbin:c04c0023…; Coursera · mobbin:18683325….
  • Provide recovery and participation context beyond the invite action. Slack shows bounce, resend and revoke states; Podia shows 'Customer invited' alongside audience progress. Neither screenshot proves an email or course was opened. Slack · mobbin:2fe9271e…; Podia · mobbin:6462b667….
  • Keep progress and the next action across devices. Coursera's web and iOS screenshots support this narrower parity claim; Udemy's mobile-only example cannot show web and mobile parity. Coursera · mobbin:f31fedca…; Coursera · mobbin:d76cce29…; Udemy · mobbin:89b6481f….

What the leading products have in common

These recommendations come from the examples cited. They are design proposals for Essentials 2.0:

  • Show course publication, audience access and item release as separate, readable choices. Podia · mobbin:94f6be70…; Circle · mobbin:ec16c800…; Kajabi · mobbin:9aa981d2….
  • Offer direct invitations and a copyable link together, with the effective permission visible before sharing. Figma · mobbin:b227715d…; Circle · mobbin:e801d8ae….
  • Provide a clearly labelled learner preview close to authoring, and say what the preview simulates. Circle · mobbin:02f1cfb0…; Canva · mobbin:5c96453b….
  • Lead the learner home with one useful next action and progress, then supporting course context. Teachable · mobbin:c04c0023…; Coursera · mobbin:18683325….
  • Separate invite status from course participation, and give instructors recovery actions. Slack · mobbin:2fe9271e…; Podia · mobbin:6462b667….

Open questions for Essentials 2.0

  • Does publication apply to all groups already assigned in module 04, or can different cohorts receive different release dates? The course, item and audience layers need an explicit rule. Teachable · mobbin:9ef7d498…; Circle · mobbin:ec16c800….
  • Should day one lead with the next simulation session, required preparation, or unfinished lessons? The self-paced homes in these screenshots support a next action but do not settle a session-based priority. Teachable · mobbin:c04c0023…; Coursera · mobbin:18683325….
  • Can instructor preview impersonate a specific group and date without changing learner completion? The enrolled-member and activity previews do not prove those safeguards. Circle · mobbin:02f1cfb0…; SchoolAI · mobbin:56f720b3….
  • When Canvas owns enrolment or the learner entry point, which system owns effective access, publication and revocation? Treat the access and status patterns as inputs; no Canvas screenshot was kept. Circle · mobbin:e801d8ae…; Podia · mobbin:94f6be70….
  • What counts as sharing success: invite accepted, first signed-in course access, or preparation started? Keep these events distinct, with report access in 08 and messages and material delivery in 09. Slack · mobbin:2fe9271e…; Podia · mobbin:6462b667….
  • Must the mobile portal support session joining, downloads and returning from an invite link? Matched Coursera course homes do not verify those end-to-end behaviours. Coursera · mobbin:f31fedca…; Coursera · mobbin:d76cce29….
What the examples cover, and how we found them

We searched Mobbin for web and iOS screens and screen sequences, and searched Refero too. The findings rest only on screenshots we kept and saved. Each screen sequence shows at most eight screens, and may be part of a longer journey. The comparison table describes these screenshots, not everything a product can do today. No example shows the whole journey from the instructor's invitation to a learner's first sign-in. We kept 28 examples across 12 products, below the target of 30 to 50, after checking relevance and image quality. We found no usable examples from Canvas, Google Classroom, Moodle, Thinkific or Maven; that is a gap in our examples, not proof they lack the feature. Most Refero results repeated Mobbin ones and were dropped, because we kept one source per product and question. The Gumroad screen we kept shows what should not count as learner access.

Three research agents (called Luna) collected the screenshots and wrote notes on them, as requested. A fourth agent (Sol) could not start because the tool's agent limit was reached. A summary agent (Astra) then combined the notes. The page uses the existing report builder, without changing the earlier module. This summary and the links to each example were added afterwards.
