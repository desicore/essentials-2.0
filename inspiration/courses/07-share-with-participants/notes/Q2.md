# Q2 — Access grant paths and permissions

Figma visibly combines direct invitation and link access in one share dialog: an email field and `Invite` button, a `Copy link` action, existing people with `can view`, and additional actions including `Copy prototype link`, `Publish to Community`, and `Get embed code`. This directly demonstrates email, link, and permission expression; it does not show a course group or access code. Ref: `mobbin:b227715d-bc5d-4655-9133-921c156f6837`.

Notion’s retained `Add members` modal asks for names or emails, offers a `Member` role with explanatory copy, and supports an invite message plus `Send invite`. This is direct email/member granting with a named permission level. Ref: `mobbin:44650693-5283-421a-b978-d4cf1a8de987`. Its second result (`mobbin:9f7bcb4e-d95c-423d-b298-f40d338e310e`) is a related guest-access state and can be cut if deduplicating.

Circle’s `Invite members` screen visibly grants permissions (`Give admin privileges`, `Make moderator of spaces/space groups`) and assigns invited members to selected spaces via a no-access/access picker, with `Send invites` as the completion action. Cross-reference its Q1 flow: Open/Private/Secret define community-member, member-gated, and invite-only space access. Refs: `mobbin:e801d8ae-1e7e-45e3-9035-0452816271e5`, `mobbin:ec16c800-3b82-4f66-8b88-76840a7590dc`.

Figma’s second screen visibly offers Anyone access, View/Edit permissions, optional password protection, and an advanced “viewers can copy, save, and export” toggle, then Save. This is explicit link-style access plus permission and password gating. Ref: `mobbin:ffaf7085-3f32-450d-938b-812274011f53`. Notion’s second screen shows a typed email suggestion labeled Guest, a Member role, invite message, and Send invite. Ref: `mobbin:9f7bcb4e-d95c-423d-b298-f40d338e310e`.



Podia (`mobbin:157502c1-8bd1-4fd3-aae9-97358b725cad`) adds a course-specific email invitation with tags and a 30-day trial warning. Gumroad (`refero:11b3c0a4-eb09-4d98-9a1a-742439189540`) is a scoped contrast: creator-side Share/Collaborators navigation does not show a learner grant.
