Mobbin captures support a clear “sent” confirmation pattern, but they do not establish opened or accepted status. Podia’s captured course audience screen ([mobbin:6462b667-d688-4634-a413-c914bdc3b20a](https://mobbin.com/flows/6462b667-d688-4634-a413-c914bdc3b20a)) shows a green “Course published!” banner, two audience rows with email addresses and progress (0/5, 1/5), and a bottom toast reading “Customer invited.” Its Analytics panel includes “Customers 2” and “Pending —”, but pending is not evidence of delivery or acceptance. The capture ends before acceptance, so it cannot support an “opened” claim.

Represent Sent, Pending, Accepted, and Opened separately. Pending must remain an unresolved state: the capture does not establish delivery. Opened is a stronger event and should only appear if the product records invite-link or email opens.

Slack’s invitation screen ([mobbin:2fe9271e-36ed-4333-b809-6078dc790098](https://mobbin.com/screens/2fe9271e-36ed-4333-b809-6078dc790098)) visibly has Requests/Pending/Accepted/Invite Links tabs; rows show “email address bounced,” “Invitation resent,” “Revoke,” and “Invitation revoked.” This is the strongest feedback analogy and a counterexample tradeoff: operational invite states are visible, but no opened state is shown.
