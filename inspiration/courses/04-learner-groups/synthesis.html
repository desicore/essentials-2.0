What the best products do differently

  • They make a group's meaning clear. Circle shows where members are inherited. Basecamp goes further and states that past assignments do not follow later group changes. Essentials needs that clarity before building synchronization. Circle mobbin:7fccd8eb-8b47-4eba-9b29-c061ef84f911; Basecamp mobbin:03d638de-71a2-4fdd-a4a9-19fb51e82492.
  • They show how many people a decision affects. Deel shows a whole-team warning and a selection count. GitBook names the exact count in its add action. Gusto separates participant selection from scheduling and review. What the leading products have in common

The patterns that repeat most are showing the count before confirming, keeping other ways to add people close by, and saying clearly what an action did. The points below are design proposals for Essentials 2.0, drawn from the cited examples rather than claims about every LMS.

  • Show a selected-learner total right before confirmation, and put the count on the enrollment action. Deel mobbin:e490327d-01e5-42c3-a4c7-c27925e5d6c1; GitBook mobbin:bb97eec3-4564-4fab-a695-555b70cf9245; Gusto mobbin:9fb5633a-b981-4130-ac7f-bddf895856ae.
  • Keep manual entry and bulk alternatives reachable from the same enrollment screen. Teachable mobbin:61127de5-672c-4250-984a-73e1c0684ce1; Partiful refero:d19d15c3-daac-4d6e-834f-a128a511a7ba; Circle mobbin:e50417f0-d6e6-4ad2-8fdc-bf7b11ff4b79.
  • Make the enrollment target and access outcomes visible before applying the change. Teachable mobbin:61127de5-672c-4250-984a-73e1c0684ce1; Asana mobbin:bb93bc5a-69d4-4f3e-8f70-fa48fb9b6b53.
  • Place course limits and the chosen full-capacity behavior where the instructor can see them. Adapt event waitlisting carefully for groups. Podia mobbin:ac312350-012f-49d9-9f95-25365859a6c3; Luma refero:4f821362-5e07-4f2a-9a8d-a63a3330ccdb.
  • State who receives an enrollment email and when. Name silent addition clearly, and keep clear failure feedback. Circle mobbin:e50417f0-d6e6-4ad2-8fdc-bf7b11ff4b79; Gusto mobbin:b1497c40-cdea-4b8a-8e73-6cd70a8fcb2f; Cloudflare mobbin:2e1d62a3-dcef-49da-b7f0-83211a076b8a.

Open questions for Essentials 2.0

  • Should attaching a semester group keep membership in sync, or copy the current roster? If it stays in sync, what happens to learner progress and recordings after removal? The examples separate inheritance from one-time copies, but they do not settle these rules. Circle mobbin:7fccd8eb-8b47-4eba-9b29-c061ef84f911; Basecamp mobbin:03d638de-71a2-4fdd-a4a9-19fb51e82492.
  • When two groups overlap, should the preview report unique learners, already enrolled learners and newly added learners separately? The kept previews show selected counts, not this breakdown. Deel mobbin:e490327d-01e5-42c3-a4c7-c27925e5d6c1; GitBook mobbin:bb97eec3-4564-4fab-a695-555b70cf9245.
  • How should instructors see group origin, direct additions and exclusions on one roster? How does an individual exception behave after a later group sync? These fields are not shown. Circle mobbin:3e4b87ce-37eb-41e8-af41-026d197eeccc; Teachable mobbin:3b608ec9-63a0-4e82-b915-4535ea70a9ac.
  • Does capacity apply to the course, a simulation event, or both? If a group will not fit, do you block the whole group, admit part of it, or waitlist the group as a unit? The examples handle course limits or individual and event waitlists.
