# Q6 — Notifications to learners on enrolment

The strongest course-enrolment evidence is Gusto’s pre-purchase summary. Its visible “What happens next” sequence is: **Gusto notifies team members** by email with course information and a link → team members complete the course in Gusto → the admin tracks progress in the dashboard (`mobbin:b1497c40-cdea-4b8a-8e73-6cd70a8fcb2f`, Gusto). This makes the notification consequence clear before **Purchase for $19.95**, but it is UI-reported future behavior; the screen does not prove an email was sent or received.

Circle provides the clearest admin control. After choosing manual entry, CSV, or an invitation link, the action menu separates **Send invites** (“Add members and send invitation emails”) from **Add without notification** (“Add members without sending invitation emails”) (`mobbin:e50417f0-d6e6-4ad2-8fdc-bf7b11ff4b79`, Circle). This demonstrates selectable notified versus silent-add intent, not actual delivery.

Cloudflare is the candidate counterexample. The invite form exposes **Skip confirmation email**, but the banner reports “Failed to invite…” because skipping confirmation is Enterprise-only (`mobbin:2e1d62a3-dcef-49da-b7f0-83211a076b8a`, Cloudflare). The failure is visible and recoverable in place; it also shows that a notification exception can block the entire invitation rather than merely alter messaging.

Asana’s project-share dialog includes **Notify when tasks are added to the project**, alongside the invite field, access settings, member list, and **Manage notifications** (`mobbin:a1406eb9-2579-4a6f-a16c-84e7f37ba4a2`, Asana). This is a neighboring ongoing-activity control, not evidence that the initial invite or course-enrolment notice is sent.

| Product | Evidence label | What is demonstrated |
|---|---|---|
| Gusto | ✓ observed | Pre-confirmation enrolment-email disclosure |
| Circle | ✓ observed | Email-invite versus silent-add choice |
| Cloudflare | partial | Skip-email exception and failed invite |
| Asana | partial | Post-access task-notification control |

No retained reference demonstrates delivery, receipt, or open state. Drop Featurebase as redundant with Circle; drop Slack because its sent-invitations view is empty and DM-specific; drop Square because waitlist availability is a different trigger and domain.
