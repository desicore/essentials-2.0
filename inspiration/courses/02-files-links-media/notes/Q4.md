# Q4 — Preview and inline playback without leaving the course

The strongest pattern is a persistent lesson shell: media plays in place while navigation and supporting material remain reachable. No capture establishes learner-side document preview.

## Product answers

- **Coursera:** Desktop embeds a full video player beside the course sequence and above Transcript, Notes, and Downloads; autoplay, quality, speed, subtitles, and fullscreen stay in the lesson ([mobbin:f91726c7-28d7-4144-8957-35666b76edf4]). Mobile keeps video above Transcript, Notes, Summary, and Attachments with Previous/Next controls ([mobbin:13ee755a-ce6f-4088-8b0e-b10c53cd2ed6]). It is the clearest course-native playback model, but neither capture previews a document.
- **Kajabi:** The learner lesson keeps large visual media, a one-lesson module outline, completion action, lesson copy, and a Downloads card on one page ([mobbin:2adb72d9-f443-4e78-a576-3c919a9c2509]). The PDF appears as a link; the image does not prove inline playback or document preview.
- **Skillshare:** The mobile course player, playback controls, lesson list, and offline-download progress share one screen ([mobbin:15e380f4-c72f-4706-b9d0-240ed6ba5ded]). A note explicitly sends learners to the **Projects** tab for course files, so materials are in-course but outside the lesson view.
- **Dropbox Dash (course-adjacent):** The three-frame flow starts in a Stack, selects an image to reveal a floating quick look, then expands it into a right-side preview pane while the Stack remains behind it ([mobbin:c899f04f-d0e2-4cfb-adfb-49a7cf92b601]). An **Open** action remains available for the source file. This is the strongest actual file-preview behavior.
- **Teachable (contextual Q1 evidence):** Its PDF flow ends with the uploaded document rendered inline in the instructor lesson editor ([mobbin:fc5a2e31-b68a-4560-adbd-64534e99d8c6]). This verifies authoring-side preview, but no learner screen shows whether the PDF remains inline after publication.

## Distinctions and counterexample

Coursera integrates playback and study tools; Kajabi composes media, outline, copy, and download link; Teachable previews an uploaded PDF for the author; Dropbox Dash progressively previews a file without losing context. **Candidate counterexample:** Skillshare keeps playback inline but directs resource access to another course tab.
