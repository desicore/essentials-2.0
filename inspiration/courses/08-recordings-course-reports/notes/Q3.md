# Q3 — Playback enrichments

**Product coverage:** Grain ✓ direct transcript/comment/timestamp evidence; Loom ✓ direct transcript/chapter evidence, partial comments (button only); Vimeo ✓ direct comments/transcript surfaces, partial enrichments (empty transcript).

**Evidence**

**Grain.** A recording detail places Summary, Chapters, and Transcript tabs beside the player. The transcript is speaker-attributed and searchable; selecting text exposes Comment and “Clip 5s.” The five captured states show transcript selection → empty comment composer → entered comment → created comment at 0:00 in both transcript and Comments panel → reply composer. A speaker timeline, plus Clips, Comments, Trackers, Metrics, and Scorecard tabs, stays adjacent to playback [mobbin:62d0f6ef-1c16-452c-8766-b3133d5bc0c7]. Text selection itself and playback seeking are not shown.

**Loom.** Two sampled playback screens show a timestamped transcript alongside video, transcript search/copy, current-segment emphasis, captions, and a Comment action [mobbin:208932bc-b2f9-4671-ac57-67868cf2d47e; mobbin:2e68b8da-81a8-4e1d-bdb8-48dec8f6e49b]. A separate edit state shows an editable Summary and Chapters list (0:00 Introduction, 1:36 First Project Discussion, 3:00 Summary & Next Steps), plus “Title, summary and chapters added” under Loom AI [mobbin:9c7d6717-30ad-411d-96a1-465961438578]. Generation, save, transcript-click seeking, and comment submission are unobserved.

**Vimeo.** One video-project screen pairs playback with collaborator comments and a “Play to comment” prompt [mobbin:a0e413d9-735c-4c11-9717-3de66d3eb960]. A public player also keeps comments adjacent [mobbin:de9410ce-93f4-435c-b9c5-b15fde660ba3]. In the editor, a Transcript panel, search, and timeline are visible, but it reports “No speech detected” [mobbin:bd482934-e96c-4560-967f-2509d3c341d8]. These are separate samples; annotation placement and navigation transitions are unobserved.

**Distinctive differences:** Grain most tightly connects transcript text to timestamped discussion; Loom visibly exposes AI chapter structure; Vimeo demonstrates collaboration and a transcript failure state.

**Recommendations for Elevate (inference):** Use AI annotation/chapterization to produce editable, timestamped scenario phases; make transcript segments and chapters seek the video; anchor instructor annotations and replies to the same timecode. Label AI output and preserve correction controls. Keep viewing/engagement signals separate from assessed mastery.

**Candidate counter-example:** Vimeo’s empty transcript state shows why speech-dependent enrichment needs a useful fallback [mobbin:bd482934-e96c-4560-967f-2509d3c341d8].

**Limitations/course applicability:** Captures do not evidence accuracy, permissions, learner visibility, or long-recording behavior. The patterns support instructor review of simulation moments, but do not establish competency assessment.
