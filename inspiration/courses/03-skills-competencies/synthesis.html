What the best products do differently

These differences mark out the strongest kept examples; they do not establish a market-wide ranking.

  1. Keep the measurement definition editable. Google Classroom puts criterion, level, description and points together. Visible makes a numeric answer readable with fixed prose, but does not let the respondent write the scale. For course setup, borrow Google Classroom's authoring structure and Visible's readable anchors.
    Google Classroom · refero:239a7ddc-e5ed-4209-87dd-f329502b2271 · Visible · mobbin:9d4ec3e3-b07c-44ea-bc93-6d661226d178
  2. Carry a named skill into an assessment. Codecademy shows subskills with Learn/Evaluate actions and repeats the subskill around the assessment. Coursera's catalog skill labels stop at saying what a course promises. The difference matters when an instructor must show what was actually measured.
    Codecademy · mobbin:d5c43b15-4d4b-4c10-9eaf-33d1e9533e44 · Codecademy · mobbin:4a76a2f1-e45c-4d90-a3aa-a30debcc9f44 · Coursera · mobbin:2d300077-7580-47c6-875a-369da8b0a065
  3. Separate evidence review from scoring. ClassDojo gives a teacher Approve/Return controls on a learner response; Codecademy shows answer outcomes and an overall score. Essentials needs both ideas, with labels that keep review status and assessed level distinct.
    ClassDojo · mobbin:67096453-158a-4b59-8633-21fcb4684817 · Codecademy · mobbin:4a76a2f1-e45c-4d90-a3aa-a30debcc9f44
  4. Show more than a progress path. Uxcel presents a score, reliability, skill dimensions and related resources. Duolingo's kept path presents lesson position and engagement signals. A learner can use a next-step path without treating it as proof of competence.
    Uxcel · mobbin:c8fcc4c3-061d-4280-90d5-4600ac48bf53 · Duolingo · mobbin:063b737c-c4d3-4788-b91d-b137f93ebb9c
  5. Let the chosen set stay easy to review. Handshake accumulates removable skill tags, then offers Save and View all. Brilliant's four-domain choice is a bounded single-choice step. Use the first pattern when an instructor is assembling a set; reserve the second for an intentionally small decision.
    Handshake · refero:8761 · Brilliant · refero:3345cd32-03fa-4571-9153-6ce930088570
  6. Make reuse an explicit action. Hello Ivy shows a template action, whereas Teachable's kept curriculum screen shows a content-import entry point. These are useful controls, but neither proves reuse of stable, versioned competency definitions across semesters.
    Hello Ivy · refero:4518 · Teachable · mobbin:5ce0dd31-4106-4884-ab40-431b863377f9

What the leading products have in common

These proposals come from patterns that repeat across the examples. Who controls the institution's list of competencies is still an open question.

  • Provide a visible review-and-save step for course configuration. Handshake makes selected skills easy to review; Google Classroom provides Save for a structured rubric.
    Handshake · refero:b8e17eec-d089-4cd1-9663-63c8b65f85ac · Google Classroom · refero:239a7ddc-e5ed-4209-87dd-f329502b2271
  • Pair numbers with words. Google Classroom offers level descriptions next to points; Visible pairs selected numbers with plain-language meanings. For simulation rubrics, write observable behavior into those descriptions.
    Google Classroom · refero:239a7ddc-e5ed-4209-87dd-f329502b2271 · Visible · mobbin:9d4ec3e3-b07c-44ea-bc93-6d661226d178
  • Make the structure easy to inspect. ClassDojo groups class skills; Codecademy shows subskills and the skill sets they build toward. Show a course's chosen competencies and their relationships before attaching activities.
    ClassDojo · mobbin:e102067a-a1a6-400a-81c4-626f7fbb066c · Codecademy · mobbin:d5c43b15-4d4b-4c10-9eaf-33d1e9533e44
  • Connect the learner's current place to a next action. Brilliant supplies a next activity; Uxcel offers learning resources for a selected competency. Keep that action separate from the label explaining what the score means.
    Brilliant · mobbin:34856cb4-4cb0-4248-ab60-d1d4bf64b665 · Uxcel · mobbin:c8fcc4c3-061d-4280-90d5-4600ac48bf53
  • Give evidence and its outcome clear identities. ClassDojo names the learner activity under review; Codecademy names the subskill around its assessment. Extend this idea in Essentials with a traceable observation, assessor and rubric version.
    ClassDojo · mobbin:67096453-158a-4b59-8633-21fcb4684817 · Codecademy · mobbin:4a76a2f1-e45c-4d90-a3aa-a30debcc9f44

Open questions for Essentials 2.0

  • Who owns the vocabulary? Handshake's entry and tag screens and Coursera's skill labels do not show who controls the list. Will Essentials distinguish institution-owned competencies from course-local skills, and who may create, rename or retire each?
    Handshake · refero:554289af-aead-4298-a222-73a19c1c1487 · Coursera · mobbin:2d300077-7580-47c6-875a-369da8b0a065
  • What makes a simulation rating comparable? Google Classroom shows editable levels and points; Visible adds a plain description to each number. How should Essentials handle checklist items, critical failures, not-observed values, assessor calibration and scale changes after scoring begins?
    Google Classroom · refero:239a7ddc-e5ed-4209-87dd-f329502b2271 · Visible · mobbin:9d4ec3e3-b07c-44ea-bc93-6d661226d178
  • What counts as evidence? ClassDojo shows a submitted response; Codecademy shows question outcomes. At the scenario or event boundary, will an assessor observation, checklist item, recording timestamp or repeated attempt support a competency score, and what can learners inspect in reports?
    ClassDojo · mobbin:67096453-158a-4b59-8633-21fcb4684817 · Codecademy · mobbin:4a76a2f1-e45c-4d90-a3aa-a30debcc9f44
  • What does reuse preserve? Hello Ivy's template action and Teachable's import entry do not show whether reused definitions stay linked or are copied. Should a new semester inherit a frozen rubric version or follow updates, and how will historical learner results remain interpretable?
    Hello Ivy · refero:4518 · Teachable · mobbin:5ce0dd31-4106-4884-ab40-431b863377f9
  • What should pass to Canvas? The kept Google Classroom rubric and ClassDojo evidence-review examples show separate pieces of data, not a connection to Canvas. Should the hand-off include competency identifiers, rubric definitions, grades, evidence links or only a summary, and which system owns later changes?
    Google Classroom · refero:239a7ddc-e5ed-4209-87dd-f329502b2271 · ClassDojo · mobbin:67096453-158a-4b59-8633-21fcb4684817
