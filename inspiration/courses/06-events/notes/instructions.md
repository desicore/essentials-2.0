# Per-question evidence review (06-events)
Read merged `../references.json` and `../assets/manifest.json`. Inspect the local images for your assigned question (Read tool on the local file; a contact sheet is fine). Root must receive compact notes, never raw search results. Write `Qn.md`, at most 350 words. Cover each product's observed answer, the flow step by step where a flow exists, what is distinctive, one candidate counter-example, and evidence gaps. Cite exact reference ids and product names. Distinguish shown behaviour from inferred behaviour: a room picker does not prove conflict detection, a capacity field does not prove a waitlist, a time-zone label does not prove per-attendee conversion. Flow previews may skip steps; do not invent missing actions. Include a compact table recommendation per product: ✓ observed / partial (analogy or incomplete evidence) / ✗ not demonstrated in this collection (not a claim the product lacks it), plus a few words. Recommend dropping irrelevant or redundant refs explicitly; do not edit references yourself. Frame findings as "how does this help an instructor scheduling simulation sessions in a course" (Semester → Courses → Events).
