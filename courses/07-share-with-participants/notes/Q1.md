# Q1 — Publish model

Teachable is the strongest visible match. Its curriculum screen shows each lesson with its own `Publish` button, while the selected lesson exposes a `Drip` panel with “# of days after student enrols,” an `Activate drip` toggle, student notification compose, and saved confirmation. This is per item visibility plus scheduled release, rather than one global switch. Ref: `mobbin:9ef7d498-63b5-4d12-a94f-5779c906f717`.

Circle’s seven screen flow is explicitly “Publishing a course space.” It shows draft mode, then an access selector with Open, Private, and Secret: Open is visible to all community members, Private to members who join or purchase, and Secret only to invited members. Later screens expose topics and thumbnail upload, ending in Save changes. Ref: `mobbin:ec16c800-3b82-4f66-8b88-76840a7590dc`.

Podia’s five screen flow shows `Course published!`, status `Published · Open · Visible`, a `Delayed 1 day` module, and an audience table with progress, signup, start, and expiry columns. Ref: `mobbin:94f6be70-d9b2-4363-8091-62d20f8d1f9e`. Kajabi’s flow shows a module menu with Draft, Publish, Drip, and Lock; choosing Publish yields Published plus a success toast. Ref: `mobbin:9aa981d2-b178-4843-b8dd-0685815ecd62`.

Candidate tradeoff: Circle makes discoverability and access mutually visible choices (Open/Private/Secret), while Podia foregrounds distribution status after publication. Cut duplicate Teachable screens `d02990dd`, `6f84d500`, `72fa1f16`, `0dcea47f`, `214c1c86` if needed.
