Refero `refero:11b3c0a4-eb09-4d98-9a1a-742439189540` ([screen](https://refero.design/pages/11b3c0a4-eb09-4d98-9a1a-742439189540)) shows Gumroad’s “Web Design Course Nº1” content editor. The top tabs are Product, Content, Receipt, and Share; the toolbar includes title, formatting, link, insert, and page controls. The left navigation separately exposes Collaborators, Checkout, Emails, Workflows, Sales, Analytics, and Payouts. This is creator-side content and collaborator management, not evidence of learner access grants, pending invites, acceptance, or opened state. It is a valid counterexample only if framed as a scope distinction: a creator may need “Share” and “Collaborators” adjacent to content, while learner invitation status belongs to an audience or enrollment surface. Otherwise it is too tangential and can be cut.
