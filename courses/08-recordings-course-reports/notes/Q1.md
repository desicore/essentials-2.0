# Q1 — Recording organisation and search

**Coverage:** Loom ✓ direct evidence; Vimeo ✓ direct evidence; Zoom ◐ partial (meeting-level detail, no library/search capture).

**Loom.** Desktop My Library combines global search (“people, tags, folders, Spaces, and Looms”), Videos/Archive/Screenshots tabs, a Training folder, and thumbnail cards carrying title, owner, age, duration, and activity signals. This supports retrieval through both hierarchy and visual/metadata scanning [mobbin:9e8909a2-43b5-4007-b72d-05475a8c0af4]. A second library sample confirms the same compact grid at a lower item count [mobbin:027d0e0e-19fe-478f-a917-0ed1fc871244]. Mobile search visibly maps a typed query (“team Mobbin”) to a title/author/age/duration result list [mobbin:d9ee7199-4790-424b-9175-3a5367d4d48f]. These are sampled screens, not one observed desktop-library → search → result sequence; no event, learner, or scenario-specific facet is visible.

**Vimeo.** Home exposes persistent Library navigation, a broad “Search videos, folders, and more” field, and a Recent videos card row with thumbnail, title, recency, duration, privacy, and item actions [refero:1429d347-5ed3-4ac6-b9d1-f906e69c23e9; refero:be4decc5-73a3-4f88-adc9-7bafc805c606]. The captures are near-duplicate dashboard states. Search submission, results, pagination, and organization by event/learner/scenario are unobserved.

**Zoom.** A recording detail groups two recordings beneath one named meeting, with meeting date/location and per-recording files (speaker view, audio, transcript), which is the clearest captured event-centric organization [mobbin:179edc0d-797b-47fa-9999-7a3998407b80]. The observed sequence is detail → Share modal → copy confirmation or recipient entry → send confirmation. Entry from a recording index and search are unobserved; sharing transitions answer an adjacent need rather than Q1.

**Distinctive difference / course use.** Loom best supports cross-library finding; Vimeo emphasizes browseable recency; Zoom preserves the session-to-artifacts bundle. For Elevate, combine course/event grouping with searchable learner and scenario metadata, while keeping completion/engagement separate from assessed mastery.

**Candidate counter-example:** Zoom’s detail-first capture: strong within-event bundling, but weak evidence for finding a recording across many courses.

**Limitations:** Captures do not prove role scope, learner-own-only access, scale behavior, or unsupported capabilities.

**Best references:** mobbin:9e8909a2-43b5-4007-b72d-05475a8c0af4 (richest organization/search surface); mobbin:179edc0d-797b-47fa-9999-7a3998407b80 (meeting-to-recording grouping and the only observed sequence).
