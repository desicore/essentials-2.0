# Q6 — Learner-side results view: same data, simplified

**Coverage:** Coursera ✓ direct evidence; Teachable partial (adjacent instructor administration, not learner-side results).

**Coursera.** The captures support a personal, task-oriented learner projection, but do not prove it is the *same underlying data* as an instructor report. The home screen combines one course card with weekly activity totals (79 items completed, 42 minutes learned), a goal, and a certificate-eligibility date (`mobbin:93beef1d-a7ba-4f6c-9efb-4d13aee3eb76`). Inside a course, completion is communicated through checked modules, congratulations, and the next course (`mobbin:15cacd44-7839-4c9f-9623-1db6833c291d`). A separate Grades screen is the strongest results evidence: overall pass and 92.52% grade, plus each assessment’s status, due date, weight, and grade (`mobbin:35b8baea-dca0-422b-83ee-01dd411da127`). The captured page labelled “assessment result” is actually a course overview with workload, learning objectives, deadlines, and upcoming graded assignments; it is planning context, not a result (`mobbin:b7931f44-6fa5-4920-b892-c9bd340e496a`). Mobile further simplifies to module completion, daily goals, due work, and content cards (`mobbin:40087b17-c119-400c-8c7a-d84d50969b19`) or a “Course Completed” state (`mobbin:cbdaad40-84eb-4881-9262-74ecb2b4ffe5`). These are isolated sampled states; navigation among dashboard, Grades, completion, and certificate is unobserved. Completion/activity should not be read as assessed mastery; only the Grades capture shows assessment performance, and none shows competency mastery.

**Teachable.** The three-step flow is instructor-side certificate management: a student enrollment at 60%, row actions, then an “Issue Certificate” modal (`refero:9615`). It does not show the learner receiving or viewing a certificate, and the captured sequence ends before confirmation or an updated table. Cull this reference from learner-side Q6 evidence, or retain only as adjacent evidence for how an instructor triggers an outcome.

**Distinctive difference / candidate counter-example.** Coursera separates motivational progress from detailed grades across responsive learner surfaces. Teachable is a useful counter-example: a per-student percentage and certificate action can look learner-specific while remaining an administrative control surface.

**Course-management implication.** Elevate can expose a compact personal summary (completion, next task, assessed results, certificate state) while keeping cohort comparison and issuance controls in instructor reports; the captures do not evidence the shared-data mapping.

**Best references:** `mobbin:35b8baea-dca0-422b-83ee-01dd411da127` for genuine learner assessment detail; `mobbin:93beef1d-a7ba-4f6c-9efb-4d13aee3eb76` for the simplified cross-course summary.
