# Q2 — Recording access control

**Product coverage:** Loom ✓ direct sharing evidence; Vimeo ✓ direct privacy/access evidence; Zoom ✓ direct sharing and expiration evidence; Teachable partial (learner roster only).

**Loom.** The share dialog accepts Spaces, names, or email addresses and can add the current workspace; its visible link state is “Anyone with the link can view.” This supports named/workspace distribution plus broad link access, but does not evidence learner-own-only access, a course group rule, or expiration. No before/after transition is captured. [mobbin:cf7c8043-a315-4bc5-a889-d3683430232c]

**Vimeo.** One privacy chooser visibly offers Unlisted, Password, Hide from Vimeo, Private (“Only you and people with access can view”), and Public. A second state shows adding a name/email, named people with Owner/Can manage/Can comment roles, and Private link privacy. Together these distinguish discovery/privacy from per-person collaboration rights. They are separate sampled screens, so the transition between them and any save outcome are unobserved. Neither capture shows expiration, a learner-own-only policy, or course-group assignment. [mobbin:e4d79f0a-e2a6-43d2-ae67-8f6019b38173] [mobbin:5262ab95-d5ee-4376-ba68-5e780dcad8a8]

**Zoom.** The share dialog exposes “Who can view” (currently everyone with the recording link) and a specific-people email field. Share Settings visibly includes an unchecked “Set expiration date,” download/transcript/registration options, a passcode, and an invitee bypass for that passcode. The four ordered previews sample share dialog → settings → altered settings → updated toast/returned share dialog; the clicks and exact persistence semantics between frames are unobserved. A specific person appears after the update, but no group selector or learner-to-own-recording binding is visible. [mobbin:156982f6-11ba-4c17-abd1-b48fc207a2e7] [mobbin:fe592646-cdd0-4bea-85cb-c69add94608f] [mobbin:ab3d0cfe-b7ab-40a8-8aaf-b4579be5f001]

**Teachable.** The Students table supports selecting learners and bulk enrolment/email/tag actions, but shows no recording or media permission control. It is only adjacent evidence for choosing course recipients. [refero:2ffee53c-1948-45bf-9485-1b57ef99632f]

**Course-management applicability.** The captures support explicit per-recording audience, named people, link privacy, and optional expiry. Elevate still needs direct evidence or product design for automatically limiting each learner to their own recording and for assigning access by learner group.

**Candidate counter-example:** Loom’s captured “Anyone with the link” state makes broad access easy for sensitive simulation footage. [mobbin:cf7c8043-a315-4bc5-a889-d3683430232c]

**Best references:** [mobbin:ab3d0cfe-b7ab-40a8-8aaf-b4579be5f001] shows the richest sampled permission-edit sequence and expiration control; [mobbin:5262ab95-d5ee-4376-ba68-5e780dcad8a8] clearly separates named roles from link privacy.
