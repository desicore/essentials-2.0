# Q2 — Preview of impact before confirming

Retain **Deel, Gusto, and GitBook**.

**Deel.** “Assign workers to course” presents **Select Workers → Schedule Assignment**. Before selection, it shows **Total 96 items**, searchable/filterable worker rows, Group/Worker status/Worker type/Job title/Contract type filters, and a disabled Continue (`mobbin:d3cc6573-05f4-4736-b9be-e419e60c7b56`). After all are selected, the same surface shows **Selected 96 of 96 items** and enables Continue (`mobbin:e490327d-01e5-42c3-a4c7-c27925e5d6c1`). Both states warn: “Just a quick check — is your whole team in?” and say workers missing from Engage settings may need access updated. This is the strongest large-impact preview, though it does not identify duplicates or already-enrolled workers.

**Gusto.** The observed flow is: (1) select a team member; **1 selected** plus name, department, job title, worker type and location are visible; (2) choose a due date and optionally enable automatic email reminders **7 days before due date**, with Preview email; (3) Review shows course name, **Number of team members: 1**, due date **Dec 1, 2025**, reminder timing, and Edit links; (4) the purchase surface shows **$19.95 per team member / 1 team member / $19.95 total** and states Gusto notifies team members after purchase (`mobbin:9fb5633a-b981-4130-ac7f-bddf895856ae`). The standalone review corroborates step 3 (`mobbin:dfee87b3-81d9-4a2f-accc-256ba91e474d`); the earlier selection screen is redundant (`mobbin:a9834b56-5fda-406a-accc-7d4ddf1b9868`). No purchase or email delivery is shown.

**GitBook.** A searchable, role-filtered modal shows **1 selected** and “Add 1 member” before commit (`mobbin:bb97eec3-4564-4fab-a695-555b70cf9245`). It is a compact count-before-action pattern, but lacks course context and conflict states.
