# Q6 — Where the step sits in course creation

**Verdict: Udemy requires course outcomes up front; the supplied Kajabi and Teachable flows do not locate an objectives step.**

Udemy places **Intended learners** first under **Plan your course**, before Course structure, Setup & test video, content creation, and Publish your course. The screen explicitly says “You must enter at least 4 learning objectives or outcomes,” while Captions and Accessibility later in the rail are labelled optional. This is direct evidence that course-level outcomes are an early required planning input, even though the course remains Draft and the screenshot does not show the exact gate triggered by leaving the page or submitting for review. [mobbin:3558619c-01e4-44de-9e1b-159104717ba1]

Kajabi shows a creation wizard generating Course Outline, Landing Page, and Email Broadcast, plus a later appearance screen where only **Thumbnail image** is explicitly optional and Back/Next remain available. No objectives/outcomes control appears, so the sequence is useful for distinguishing core generation from optional polish but cannot establish whether objectives are required, optional, implicit in the outline, or editable later. [mobbin:2e01c0b8-2b25-491e-a7d1-992341cc9269; mobbin:b7491347-c116-4aad-950c-8d48f577b98a]

Teachable’s course Setup guide groups curriculum, customization, and pricing before a course-level Publish action. In the sampled three-frame section flow, the instructor opens Quick actions → Publish all and receives a published toast. In the sampled four-frame course flow: Setup guide → publish confirmation → launch success → Published course. These frames place publishing after curriculum work, but neither flow shows objectives or proves an objective-field requirement. [mobbin:2da07b87-105e-4850-8686-9cad55f3f455; mobbin:757f57cb-e9e7-4355-a542-db3033f23dc0; mobbin:ca328d45-4fee-42c1-925b-ff8e298dc4b8; mobbin:1f84e7ef-b3c7-4b32-a5c9-29796bf44de6]

**Counterexample/gap:** Teachable can reach visible publish confirmation without any objective step in the sampled flow, a candidate counterexample to universal up-front objectives—not proof that the product lacks another gate. The school checklist and AI outline screen should be dropped for Q6: neither evidences objective placement. Optional thumbnail evidence must not be generalized to outcomes.
