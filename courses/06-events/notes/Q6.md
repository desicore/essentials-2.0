# Q6 — Time zone and duration handling

**Cal.com** (refero:3492 reschedule flow; mobbin:8a0d881c availability screen): time zone is an explicit dropdown (Europe/London on the booking page; Asia/Jakarta on the organizer's Availability page), duration on reschedule is inherited from the original booking (15m), not re-editable — shown, not selectable, at that step.

**Clockwise** (mobbin:1fe20459, 2-frame flow): picker → confirm modal. Zone is an explicit dropdown under the calendar (GMT+07:00 Asia/Jakarta); duration is a fixed preset label ("30 minutes"), not chosen by the booker.

**Dialpad** (mobbin:a9dbeaf9, single screen): date/time fields plus a zone chip ("PST") and a duration preset dropdown ("45 min").

**Homerun** (refero:7186, flow): New-event modal shows start time + a duration dropdown ("60 min") next to it (start+duration model, end implied); the zone appears only as a read-only label ("EUROPE/TALLINN") — no dropdown control visible.

**Microsoft Teams** (refero:9746, flow): New-event form leads with an explicit zone dropdown (UTC+09:00 Osaka/Tokyo), then separate start/end date+time fields with a computed duration readout ("30m") and an all-day toggle.

**Kajabi** (mobbin:5de887fe) and **SavvyCal** (mobbin:9a8fc4f4): both pair an explicit zone dropdown with duration presets and a live availability grid — near-duplicate patterns.

**Upwork** (mobbin:55d0a62f, 2 frames): scheduling modal has zone dropdown + duration preset, but distinctively the chat header independently shows the counterpart's live local time and offset ("2:16 PM GMT+8, 2h ahead") — the only explicit other-party-local-time preview in this set.

**Zoom** (mobbin:f6032c78): zone dropdown plus free hr/min duration entry (not a preset list) — a counter-example to the preset-dominant pattern.

**Counter-example candidate**: Homerun (refero:7186) — no visible zone selector, only an inferred read-only label, contradicting the majority "explicit dropdown" pattern.

**Evidence gaps**: no frame shows an invitee-side converted-time confirmation or email; single-screenshot refs (Dialpad, Kajabi, SavvyCal, Zoom, Cal.com availability) lack a full flow context.
