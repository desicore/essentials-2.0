# Research prompt — Courses › Announcements / Send learning materials or send to canvas → Shareable link

**Priority sub-module (highlighted on the module map).**

Using Mobbin & Refero MCP, research how top apps design **course announcements and one-to-many distribution of learning materials** (post an announcement to a course, send selected materials to learners or groups, generate a shareable link, push to an external LMS like Canvas; scheduling, targeting and read receipts). Find the most relevant screens and user flows, compare patterns across products, and identify what the best apps do differently. Create a visual HTML report (images embedded directly) showing the top examples, common patterns, and the best practices top companies converge on. Keep the big picture in mind: this is one sub-module of **managing courses** in Essentials 2.0 (Semester → Courses → this → Shareable link).

## What the instructor is trying to do here
Communicate from inside the course: announcements to all / a group, sending specific files or links (from 02) ahead of a session (06), pre-reading, post-session debrief material; choose channel (in-portal, email, shareable link, Canvas LMS post); schedule; see who received / opened. The "send to canvas" branch means an integration hand-off — look for "post to LMS / export to Google Classroom / share to Slack" patterns too.

## Search queries to start from
Mobbin `search_screens` (web, deep):
- "create announcement composer for a course with audience selector and schedule option"
- "announcements feed inside a course with pinned posts and comments"
- "share materials modal choosing recipients groups and sending via email or link"
- "share link settings with expiration, access level and copy button"
- "post to external platform integration picker like Google Classroom or Slack"
Mobbin `search_flows` (web, then ios):
- "instructor posts an announcement to a class and schedules it"
- "sharing a document by generating a link with permissions and sending it"
- "exporting or publishing content to a connected LMS"
Refero: same intents; also Google Classroom (Stream + Share to Classroom), Canvas (Announcements), Moodle (Forum announcements), Slack (post to channel, scheduled send), Notion (publish to web), Loom (share), Google Drive (share link), Mailchimp / Substack (schedule & audience), Kahoot / Quizizz (share to Classroom/Teams).

Products to prioritise: Google Classroom, Canvas, Moodle, Teachable, Thinkific, Kajabi, Circle, Slack, Notion, Loom, Google Drive, Mailchimp, Kahoot, Quizizz.

## Questions the board answers (Q1–Q6: these become the H2 sections of the report and the columns of the comparison table)
1. Composer: audience targeting (course / group / individual), attachment of course materials, scheduling
2. Channel choice (in-app feed, email, push, link, external LMS) and whether it is one composer or many features
3. Shareable link controls (expiry, access level, revoke, tracking)
4. Delivery feedback (sent / delivered / seen, reminders)
5. External LMS hand-off UX (auth, mapping to course, what is sent, confirmation)
6. Learner-side reception (feed, notification, email) and consistency with the portal
