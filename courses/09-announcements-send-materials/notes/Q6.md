# Q6 — Learner-side reception (feed, notification, email) and consistency with the portal

## Per product

- **Circle** `mobbin:c1b1c3ad` — "Announcements" is its own sidebar entry, separate from the general "Feed," so an admin's announcement is routed to a dedicated space rather than only appearing mixed into ordinary feed content; a bell/inbox/bookmark row sits in the header as the notification surface.
- **ClassDojo** `mobbin:3edf67a5` — A Class Story post shows an attachment (photo) and an existing reply inline beneath the announcement text, with Like/Comment actions on the post itself, plus a teacher-only Pin/Edit/Delete menu so a course runner can keep material surfaced at the top of what families see.
- **Coursera** `mobbin:36db9592` — weak reference, see below.
- **Microsoft Teams** `mobbin:88ad5199` — The composer offers two explicit post types, "Post" and "Announcement"; a posted Announcement renders with a bold title distinct from ordinary chat posts, with inline "View conversation"/"Reply" keeping comments attached to the original message.
- **Slack** `mobbin:8e65f9c9` — A dedicated Activity screen aggregates unread state as numbered badges across DMs, Mentions, and channels ("5 new" catch-up tile, per-item counts), tracking what's unread centrally rather than only inside the source channel.
- **Spotify** `refero:471b8f0c` — A notification-settings screen lets a user set Email and Push independently per content category, modeling per-category channel control a learner could apply to course communications.
- **Teachable** `mobbin:5a524cf6` — weak reference, see below.

## Distinctive

- Explicit "Announcement" as a separate, visually bolded post type in the composer — Microsoft Teams `mobbin:88ad5199` only.
- Teacher-facing Pin control exposed directly on the post via a menu — ClassDojo `mobbin:3edf67a5` only.
- Per-category Email vs. Push toggles — Spotify `refero:471b8f0c` only.
- Numbered unread badges aggregated across multiple source types in one inbox screen — Slack `mobbin:8e65f9c9` only.

## Counter-example candidate

`mobbin:36db9592` (Coursera): a technical-support question, a bug report, and a lecture question sit together, undifferentiated, in one "Latest"-sorted list with no pin, no unread marker, and no sender-role indicator. In a course portal, a genuine instructor announcement dropped into this stream would be visually indistinguishable from peer chatter and could scroll out of view.

## Weak references

- `mobbin:36db9592`: shows a discussion/Q&A thread list, not an announcement or materials-delivery view; no unread, pin, or channel-preference elements visible.
- `mobbin:5a524cf6`: visible tab is a generic community "Activity" log (forum replies/topics), not an announcements or shared-materials inbox; the Notifications/Preferences tabs the take references are not the tab actually shown.

## Take fixes

- `mobbin:c1b1c3ad`: Circle sidebar shows "Announcements" as a separate nav item from "Feed," with a notification bell in the header; the visible feed posts show no pin badge or comment count.
- `mobbin:36db9592`: Mobile Coursera discussion list sorted by Latest, mixing a bug report, a technical question, and a lecture-content question with reply/upvote counts — a Q&A forum view, not an announcement feed.
- `mobbin:5a524cf6`: Teachable community "My Posts → Activity" tab lists recent forum posts/replies by category (General, Staff); Notifications and Preferences are adjacent tabs, not shown here.
