# Q5 — External LMS hand-off UX (auth, mapping to course, what is sent, confirmation)

## Per product

- **Circle** `mobbin:d73f14de-928b-46cb-8011-e5959e5b672d` — (b) internal publish. A lesson's status field is flipped Draft → Published inside Circle's own lesson editor; no external system is named or contacted. Steps: lesson list (all Draft) → open lesson editor → set Status to Published → "Changes saved" toast. Tells a course manager nothing about handing content to an outside LMS.
- **Cofounder** `mobbin:7897406d-dd60-4afa-94ad-65a9468c1d64` — (c) generic integration connect, no content sent. It's a social cross-poster, not an LMS. Steps: destination picker (LinkedIn/Instagram/X/Facebook/Reddit/TikTok) → Instagram OAuth permission grant (per-scope toggles) → "continue sharing" re-consent screen → "loading available accounts" → back to destination picker ("finish connecting in the popup"). Flow never reaches a send or success state.
- **Craft** `mobbin:e9079303-b68b-4a54-98b0-0ba587e8ba7d` — (c) generic integration connect, no content sent. Settings panel lists dev-tool integrations (Figma, Github, Jira, Linear) with a bare Connect/Disconnect toggle; no destination selection, no content, no confirmation of anything sent.
- **Kajabi** `mobbin:9aa981d2-b178-4843-b8dd-0685815ecd62` — (b) internal publish. Draft→Publish dropdown on modules inside Kajabi's own curriculum outline, ending in "Successfully updated module" toast. No external course or LMS involved.
- **SchoolAI** `mobbin:3135010e-a999-439d-8ebd-50e7f4a58dcc` — (a) real external hand-off, only one that shows the full loop. Steps: Export menu lists Google Classroom alongside Docs/Forms/Word/PDF → permission-request modal explaining what will be shared → Enable (OAuth) → "Add to Google Classroom" modal with a Class picker to map the document to a specific class ("AS Kindergarten") → Add to Google Classroom → "Exported to Google Classroom!" toast.
- **Teachable** `mobbin:757f57cb-e9e7-4355-a542-db3033f23dc0` — (b) internal publish. "Quick actions → Publish all" on curriculum lessons within Teachable itself, confirmed by "Published all lessons in Introduction" toast. No external destination.

## Distinctive

- SchoolAI is the only reference with a course/class-mapping step (Class dropdown) tying the sent content to a specific destination roster.
- Cofounder is the only one showing granular per-permission OAuth toggles (profile, comments, messages, publish, insights) and a "you previously connected — continue sharing?" re-consent screen.
- Craft is the only one reducing "connect" to a single settings-row toggle with zero content or destination context.

## Counter-example candidate

`mobbin:7897406d-dd60-4afa-94ad-65a9468c1d64` (Cofounder) — the flow stalls at a "finish connecting in the popup" loading state and never shows what was actually posted or any completion state. A course manager relying on this pattern would authorize access but get no evidence of what reached the destination or confirmation it arrived.

## Weak references

- `mobbin:d73f14de-928b-46cb-8011-e5959e5b672d` — take claims "confirmation of distribution" but the screens only show an internal Draft/Published toggle, not a hand-off.
- `mobbin:9aa981d2-b178-4843-b8dd-0685815ecd62` — take implies content "sent to learners" but it's an internal status change with no external system.
- `mobbin:757f57cb-e9e7-4355-a542-db3033f23dc0` — take implies confirmation of "what content is sent" but it's a bulk internal publish toggle.

## Take fixes

- `mobbin:d73f14de-928b-46cb-8011-e5959e5b672d`: Internal Draft/Published status toggle for a lesson inside Circle's own editor — no external LMS or destination is involved.
- `mobbin:7897406d-dd60-4afa-94ad-65a9468c1d64`: OAuth connect flow for cross-posting to social platforms, with granular permission toggles and a destination picker, but the flow ends before showing what was sent or any success state.
- `mobbin:e9079303-b68b-4a54-98b0-0ba587e8ba7d`: Settings-page list of dev-tool integrations (Figma, Github, Jira, Linear) with a bare Connect/Disconnect toggle — no content selection or send confirmation.
- `mobbin:9aa981d2-b178-4843-b8dd-0685815ecd62`: Internal Draft→Publish status change for a course module inside Kajabi itself — no external system involved.
- `mobbin:3135010e-a999-439d-8ebd-50e7f4a58dcc`: Complete external hand-off — export menu offers Google Classroom, a permission-grant modal, a class picker mapping the document to a specific class, and a toast confirming the export.
- `mobbin:757f57cb-e9e7-4355-a542-db3033f23dc0`: Internal bulk "Publish all" action for curriculum lessons inside Teachable — no external LMS hand-off shown.
