# Q4 — Sequencing after add

## Product approaches

- Circle keeps section and lesson ordering in one authoring outline. The five-frame flow visibly changes from four to five lessons, then lifts the complete “Culinary Explorations” section and places it above “The World of Potatoes”; lesson membership and Draft labels remain intact (`mobbin:0489e85e-877c-41fc-92dc-6e6e7bc9c7da`).
- Kajabi combines a searchable four-module outline with per-module “Add Content,” expand/collapse, and lesson-level Draft, Publish, Drip, and Lock badges. The screenshot establishes grouped state visibility, but no reorder or prerequisite-setting action (`mobbin:5b4497cf-d3c9-445f-bff6-754fbbb1cf7c`).
- Podia places “New lesson” and “New section” above grouped lesson rows. A later module is labeled “Delayed 1 day”; this is direct timing evidence, not evidence of an item prerequisite (`mobbin:0aed2e1a-0de0-4f32-9dba-2d5a627a1e87`).
- Teachable shows section cards with lesson rows, “New lesson,” “Bulk upload,” and “Section summary.” Its open Drip panel targets one section and offers release on a date or a number of days after enrollment. Grip icons are visible, but the single frame does not demonstrate reordering (`mobbin:e84f7cdf-c9b2-4a3d-bddf-31e53e19e509`).
- Udemy presents a learner-facing curriculum: 14 sections and 88 lectures, with the first section expanded into numbered lessons and later sections collapsed. It demonstrates a scannable published hierarchy only (`mobbin:045c215d-6e08-432c-9bb6-3ddd4495fc69`).

## Visible step sequence

Circle alone supplies a post-add sequence: initial two-section outline → a fifth lesson appears under “Culinary Explorations” → the section is shown lifted with both lessons → it moves above the other section → the reordered outline settles with both groups preserved (`mobbin:0489e85e-877c-41fc-92dc-6e6e7bc9c7da`).

## Scope limitation

Udemy’s published hierarchy is a learner-view analogue, not evidence of instructor sequencing. None of the retained frames demonstrates an item-prerequisite editor. MasterClass discovery was reassigned to Q1 during curation.
